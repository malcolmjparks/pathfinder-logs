The Wrath of the Stormbringer

Return to Arkham

The city is still experiencing fierce storms. Questioning the locals, the team finds out that a mysterious figure wreathed in storm and shadow has been gathering Ithaqua's followers. They tell Seamus O'Bannion about Adelei, and he suggest meeting with Keith David at the diner, who was Adelei's assistant and protégé. Discussing what they think Adelei's plans may be, they are suddenly fired upon by four cultists. The investigators kill three of them, and knock the last of them to the ground. They try to intimidate him and get information out of him - he mostly babbles incoherently about the coming of the great one, but they do discern the location where Adelei is performing the final ritual to finally free Ithaqua and turn himself into an avatar of the Wind-Walker (Mount Graylock) and find out that the ritual is happening soon.

Between them, they also think that there may be a way to sever Adelei's connection to the shard of the storm by disrupting the ritual - though not what the fallout from that may be.

The Climb to Mount Graylock

Driving to Mount Graylock, the investigators find the mountain covered with a storm and are disturbed by Adelei's voice whispering taunts to them as they start the climb. The going is very tough, and the conditions take their toll with Amos getting injured and all the team suffering the horror of Adelei's words. Halfway up the mountain they encounter a pack of wendigos, which prove tough but fall with the help of Keith casting Azure Flame at each of them.

The Ritual of Storm and Blood

At the top of the mountain, they find Adelei standing upon a frozen altar, wreathed in crackling energy. Their body is half-human, half-storm and their eyes glow with inhuman fury. Snow and wind swirl unnaturally around them, and the fabric of reality itself seems thinner here. With the ritual started, McG and Keith do their best to disrupt it while Peter and Dan attack Adelei to try to distract them.

Adelei pushes on with the ritual and starts casting spells to attack the investigators, and manages to turn incorporeal to frustrate the efforts of Peter and Dan. Keith, Mcg and Amos do manage to do enough to start to disrupt the ritual. Adelei becomes solid again and fights back, but at that moment Adelei's second attempt to turn incorporeal goes wrong and Peter and Dan take this opportunity to inflict multiple injuries on him. Finally Dan rushes up in slow motion and decapitates Adelei, much to Keith's horror.

As Adelei's body explodes in a magical burst, the investigators have a vision of an angry Ithaqua trying to escape - but at that moment a rumbling voice overwhelms them and they hear "That is not dead which can eternal lie, And with strange aeons even death may die." and they see Ithaqua's bindings reappear around its limbs.

With the storm abating and the sun appearing for the first time in quite a while, the team head back to Arkham to see what the fallout will be - and try not to think about what they may have woken...

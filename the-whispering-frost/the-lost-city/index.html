The Lost City

Through the Gate

Stepping through the gate, the investigators find themselves inside a crumbling stone temple. The air is freezing cold, but they feel warmth emanating from the stonework at their feet, which also glows with a strange blue light. Strange plants also grow from cracks in the stonework, and they can see through the archway leading outside that the evening sky is lit with the Aurora Borealis. Amos mentioned that Hyperborea is known as the land of eternal twilight.

McG and Amos investigate the carvings on the wall, and they deduce that Hyperborea was founded because of the imprisonment of Ithaqua by the Ancient Ones, and was populated by the servants of the Ancient Ones who did their bidding. This room was obviously a gateway between Hyperborea and Earth and was at one point a commonly used way-station. As they are exploring, they make enough noise that two living statues come to interrupt. They mindlessly attack whoever is nearest - but Dan Pacho guards everyone while they try to work out what to do. Bullets and Shotguns seem to do little damage, but Dan's brass knuckles do the job along with McG smashing them with broken pieces of stone and Peter using his baseball bat.

Adelei examines their broken pieces and works out that they were once ordinary statues that have been corrupted and forced to serve Ithaqua's will. Stepping out into the city, the investigators see that they are on the edge of a large bowl, with ruined temples all around the bowl in front of them - and the largest at the bottom of the bowl but reaching up high into the sky.

The Fall of Hyperborea

The map that McG has pieced together shows the location of the Hall of Records - the team head that way, though the streets are confusing and the investigators have to scramble over and around various objects along the way, with several taking some injuries along the way. At the hall of records, Adelei find reference to The Shard of the Storm which seems to be the lock that was used to imprison Ithaqua. The shard is kept under the temple at the centre of the city, with Ithaqua's prison chamber above it.

McG and Amos find more information about how and why Hyperborea fell - in their hubris, the residents of Hyperborea tried to leech Ithaqua's power to empower the city. It backfired when the inhabitants became corrupted and mutated into creatures that served Ithaqua's will. They find notes detailing how the last of Hyperborea's inhabitants fled through the gate to Greenland whilst being pursued by the creatures, which McG recognised. These mutated creatures are like the Messenger that they encountered in the cave near the ritual site.

At this moment, Dan raises the alarm as a Messenger creeps up to attack him. Peter is not so lucky and is taken by surprise (though Dan's shout warns him a little). Dan shows his keen machete skills as he brutally dismembers one of the beasts, while the other investigators all focus on the other, eventually managing to kill it. They then head towards the temple at the centre of the city.

The Shard of the Storm

At the temple of forgotten gods, the investigators head down to try to find the shard of the storm. They hear chanting coming from an underground chamber, and they see a group of cultists performing a ritual around a large onyx stone with lightning bolts constantly flashing inside it. Dan tries to sneak up, but makes a noise at the last moment giving himself away and the the cult leader dodges his machete blow. However this only earns him brief respite as he is shot by Pete and McG, and burnt by Adelei's azure flame. Blinded, he casts storm of spirits - which damages everyone, but he is then swiftly dispatched with the other cultists killed soon after.

While McG and Amos discuss whether to use the Shard of the Storm to rebind Ithaqua, or to destroy it to prevent him from using it to travel to Earth, Adelei decides to try to use the stone's power to gain control of storms. He succeeds, but at a terrible cost - he has lost his humanity and sanity in one fell blow. Cackling madly and with a swirling storm surrounding him, Adelei flies out of the temple, and the investigators run outside in time to see him fly into the portal chamber and head back to earth.

Heading back that way themselves, they meet up with Erik on the other side and he says he saw something emerge from the gate and fly off. He seems disconcerted, but leads the team back to Ivittuut, where they eventually find a ship to take them back to North America and transport back to Arkham. When they get back to the city, not all is as it was when they left.

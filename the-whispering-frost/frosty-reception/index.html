Frosty Reception

The Idols' Call

The investigators can still hear the noise of the storm outside. They question Seamus about any mysterious happenings in the last few weeks, and whether there was any unknowns in the club. He recalls a tall man with dark hair who seemed to smile too much. He played cards in the card room, so the team head there to search. Meanwhile, they hear more wendigos trying to break in the back entrance. Seamus and a couple of heavies try to shore up the defenses.

As McG and Adelei find a third black wooden idol and they pick it up. At this moment, another Cold Shadow attacks as at the same time they hear a group of wendigos break in through the back door, and Seamus shouts for assistance.

The team split up, with Dan running to help Seamus. After destroying the Cold Shadow, the team rescue Seamus and kill the remaining wendigos. Amos heals everyone, and Adelei casts his spell to get a vision of Hyperborea - unfortunately this time things go a little differently. Adelei seems to be pulled in to the chamber where Ithaqua is imprisoned, and he witnesses Ithaqua break the last of the chains binding him. Meanwhile, the rest of the investigators see Adelei's eyes shine bright blue, and a deep voice emanates from his mouth as he says to the others "Thank you for your assistance my friends." Adelei sees a giant hand reach down to grab him... but at that moment he is returned to his body.

Destroying the Evidence

Holding the three wooden idols and the onyx idol, McG and Amos work out what must have happened.

  • Clarence Sheldon must have found the idols (and maybe some of the strange coins) and knew enough about them to try a ritual to empower them and sabotage the O'Bannions.
  • It was a partial success, but his mind began to be taken over by Ithaqua. He returned to Arkham and paid someone to plant one idol in the Clover Club, then he planted one on a smuggling route used by the O'Bannions and let one be "captured" by Grady.
  • The plan of Ithaqua and the Messenger seemed to be to find people who are not associated with Ithaqua to charge the onyx idol and help free his master. This may be because the onyx idol is a "key" used by the Ancient Ones when they were rumoured to have imprisoned Ithaqua after he rebelled.
  • Having the onyx idol and the three wooden idols in one place was part of the plan, as the wooden idols drew beasts like wendigos close and the onyx idol then stole their spirit.
  • Ithaqua has used the idol to free his chains, but he is still trapped in his prison chamber.
  • Both the coins and the idols are relics imbued with some of Ithaqua's power, and dangerous to keep close.

With this information, the team decide to burn the idols and melt the coins. They have the bodies of four cultists in their van as well, which they need to get rid of - Seamus has some contacts at a nearby factory with a furnace where they sometimes pay to make problems "disappear". He gives the team money to cover the necessary bribes and a bit extra for saving his life.

A Message from the North

At the library of the Miskatonic University, the team follow up on leads on Hyperborea and any information that they can find about how to get there. In an obscure tome, they find references to entrances in Greenland and Alaska. The team is initially split as to which route to follow, but they eventually decide on Greenland. They take the train up to Newfoundland and pay for some space on a trading vessel heading to Greenland.

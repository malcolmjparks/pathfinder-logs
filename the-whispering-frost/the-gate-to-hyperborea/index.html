The Gate to Hyperborea

Arrival in Greenland

The investigators had booked a berth in a trading vessel heading to Greenland. They help out on the boat and do some fishing. Adelei manages to cook one of the best meals the crew has ever tasted.

Arriving in the key silver mining town of Ivittuut, the team heads to the local inn to find somewhere to stay for the night, and hopefully find out what to do next. The innkeeper tells them that an expedition team arrived a week or so before, and headed off towards some old ruins after getting some directions from the locals. They seemed to be well-equipped and brought all their own sleds etc. with them, rather than hiring locally. However the locals have said that over the last couple of days, they have seen frozen bodies moving about in the distance, and they are worried.

Adelei manages to cook another decent meal, so the innkeeper lets him help out that evening. The innkeeper calls over Erik Aunartoq, a local guide who offers to help equip the team and guide them to the ruins.

Their sleep is disturbed by shouts and shots from outside - rushing out, they see a group of frozen bodies attacking the locals, who are firing pistols at them (including Erik). Dan Pacho rushes up with his machete to protect Erik, and the others fire their weapons, and they finish the zombies off. Searching the bodies, they find a symbol that they previously found on the cultists in Amos's study - as well as a fragment of a mysterious Hyperborean map.

The Glacier Trail

Heading off on a pair of sleds pulled by huskies, after several hours they see a crashed sled off to one side. It looks like some of the cultists in the previous expedition crashed and were abandoned, with some trying to head back to town and becoming frozen zombies. McG picks up some spare boots and gloves. Adelei gets the feeling that something is watching the group.

Speeding along, Erik spots a crack forming and steers away to avoid it. Peter steers the other sled away as well, as they hear ice cracking below them and an Ice-Wraith emerging. They fire guns at it, but do only slight damage. Adelei casts Azure Flame on it, which does a lot more damage - although he repeatedly sets himself on fire as well.

Once the wraith is defeated and disappears into nothingness, the team press on through the storm towards the ruins.

The Hidden Ruins

After a couple of days travel with no more incident, they arrive at the site of the ruins. McG and Amos are very excited by the Hyperborean runes on some of the pillars, and deduce that they hold instructions to activate a gate in an ancient archway at the centre of the ruins. Adelei tries to gain a vision of Hyperborea, but this time sees a vision of the other side of the gate, looking back at himself on the Greenland side.

At this moment, a group of people approach - the lead man introduces himself as Dr. Elias Coldbrook and demands that they hand over the onyx idol to him immediately. He claims that Ithaqua's escape is inevitable, but that if they hand over the idol he would be able to control the Wind-Walker. When the team refuse, he grows angry and instructs his men to take the idol by force.

Amos and McG take cover behind some pillars, and McG shoots his Tommy gun towards the cultists. Peter blasts one of the goons with his shotgun, and Peter attacks with his machete, temporarily blinding Elias. Elias tries to cast Storm of Spirits, but with his vision impaired he fails and moments later is severely injured. With the other cultists soon despatched, Elias curses the team and laments that the Earth will be destroyed. McG finds the other half of the map and a journal of Erik's research on Hyperborea and the onyx idol, among other things.

The team decide to open the gate to Hyperborea and travel through - leaving Erik behind to look after the huskies. He says that if they are not back in four days, he will have to head back to Ivittuut without them. Adelei casts a spell to open the portal in the archway, and an Ice Colossus rushes out - however it is met with a barrage of bullets, a shotgun blast and finally a machete to its weak spot which shatters it. McG says "Welcome to Earth!"

The investigators step through the gate and are surprised at what they find on the other side.

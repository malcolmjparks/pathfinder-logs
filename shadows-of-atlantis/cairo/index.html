    Cairo

    Act One: Reflections

    According to Ehrlichmann's notes, the original stone is in the Cairo Museum of Antiquities. He also suggests that a local retired Army Engineer, a Major Aloysius Blythe-Manders is worth talking to. After asking around at some bars frequented by military types, they are directed to his home. They are met at the door of the compound by a small man with stunningly bright blue eyes. He takes them to the Major, who welcomes them and offers them food and drinks. He shows them some of his collection of historical artifacts, which he is very proud of. When Garance mentions the museum, the Major offers to write them a letter of introduction.

    Meanwhile, Calvin is exploring the garden, where he sees the small man - Sheikh Janin El-Rauf - digging. He is obviously taking great pains to show the tattoo on his wrist of a spiral pattern. He speaks to Calvin in a mix of Arabic, English and French. It's clear he wants the agents to come back at night with the flowers of the blue lotus.

    At the markets, they buy some of the blue lotus flowers then move on to the museum. They are met by M. Lucien Kutay who tells them about his work updating the museum catalogue. He hasn't seen anything matching the description of the black stone, but he directs them to the card index from just after the Great War. After a bit of searching, they find a card that seems to reference what they are looking for. A bit more persuasion is needed to convince him to let them into the storeroom, but eventually he relents and he accompanies them there. They find the crate with the stone in, and take a fresh rubbing, noticing the same spiral pattern overlaying the text.

    That night, the agents head back to the Major's property - but are accosted by street thugs on the way, who they swiftly overcome. At the property, the Sheikh lets them in and motions for them to be silent. He removes the cover from the well with Calvin's help, and he explains that the power of the sun has entered the Duat (underworld) and lies there waiting for them to free it. They must find a doorway to the land of the night, and the Well of Stars can grant a vision to help. Louis takes an ostrich feather from him and draws the spiral pattern in the well as the Sheikh chants in the background.

    In the well they see a sandstone facade supported by six pillars; each of the pillars contains the carved head of a woman with cow's ears: the goddess Hathor. The ruins to the right of the temple split and the sun begins to rise through the crack.

    The Sheikh takes back his feather, and covers the well. He also lets them know in broken Arabic, English and French:

    • The temple they saw is near Qena on the opposite bank of the Nile.
    • The ruin was the Sanatorium, where the sick would come to find cures through prophetic dreams induced by narcotic beer.
    • The agents must make their own journey into the Duat and face the challenges - if their hearts are found to be in balance and they defeat the dread Apophis, the sun will be theirs.

    He volunteers to make the "red ale of truth" for them, but tells them to come back and collect it in the morning. When they collect it, he says "Keep your metau sharp, and do not be chained by thinking that willow is only of use for making cricket bats."

    Act Two: Into Darkness

    The journey to Qena will take a couple of days by paddle steamer up the Nile. Knowing that they want to come back incognito, the agents arrange for a truck on the way back. They also research The Book of Gates, which details the journey of the barge of the Sun through the Duat. It mentions a metau as a forked stake, and that Apep-Apophis is defeated with five sets of chains.

    Travelling up the Nile, they see a large archaeological dig near Amarna, which Calvin confirms is Nachtwolfe - with their mistranslated text, they are digging in the wrong place! He also spots a monk in blue robes at the dig site.

    Arriving in the town of Dendera near Qena, it is a short 2 mile camel ride to the temple. They pay for a guide and camels to take them and they spend the day looking around the temple and surrounding ruins. As evening falls, they talk to the local watchmen and offer them a little baksheesh to look the other way as they camp out in the Sanatorium. Their guide has also been paid to watch over them as they sleep, so they drink the red ale.

    The agents wake on a wide stone pavement with the sun sinking behind two peaks. Louis immediately recognises this as a pocket of the Dreamlands, and warns the others that time and space act differently here. There is nothing else around them, so they walk towards the mountains, which looked like a journey of a couple of hours. They reach them in a couple of minutes.

    A massive gateway is before them, with mummies and a huge snake guarding it. The snake asks each of them clearly "Who are you and why do you wish to travel through the Duat?" Louis answers truthfully and the snake moves on to Ralph, who lies and suddenly wakes up back in the Sanatorium. Garance and Calvin decide to tell the truth, and the gate opens up for them.

    At the next gate, another snake guardian sits in front of a board - Louis recognises it as a game of Senet, a game notorious for having clear rules in the Dreamlands that nobody can remember in the waking world. They beat the guardian on the first attempt, who congratulates them and opens the gate.

    The next chamber has a large set of scales in the centre, with a feather on one side. The carvings on the wall make it clear that people sit on the scales. Calvin goes first, and though the scales swing a little, he ends up in balance. Garance has a little trouble getting up on the scales, but after both she and Louis weigh themselves, a secret door opens and they can pass through.

    The path is lined with jackal-headed standards, and the agents are disturbed to see the dead bodies of everyone who has ever crossed them, their heads moving to follow them as they walk. They reach a black lake with a small barque tied up to a jetty. The shore is lined with willow trees, and Louis leads the way in fashioning metau and a set of five chains.

    The journey across the lake to the island in the centre is easy, and Louis's dog is happy at the prow of the barque. As they tie it up to the island jetty, the waters of the lake burst into flame. Ahead of them is a temple gate, and as they approach there is a loud sound and a huge worm-like creature erupts from the ground in front of them, almost knocking Garance over. Louis tries to distract it while the others bind it with the chains. The massive creature entirely misses them when trying to attack, and after five chains have bound it, it sinks into the ground.

    Inside the temple there are four figures, each holding a variation of a sun-disc in front of them. Calvin doesn't know how to choose, but Garance remembers a carving from Major Blythe-Manders' house and picks the correct disc. As she picks it up, the other two notice a blue monk watching them as they wake up.

    Act Three: Getting away with it

    With Nachtwolfe almost certainly now aware that the agents have the artifact, they travel back to Dendera and load into the truck. Louis drives and they avoid detection for most of the journey. As they are approaching Cairo however, two Mercedes-Benz cars roar up, and one of them tries to ram the truck. Louis speeds up as Ralph throws a grenade under one of the cars, spinning it around and leaving it behind. Both the agents and the Nachtwolfe try to shoot each other's tyres out, and Garance tries to trick the second car into driving into a ramp, but then Calvin snipes the driver of the second car. It veers off and crashes into a wall. Realising that Cairo is probably too hot for them now, the agents swiftly make arrangements to fly back to England.

    Meeting up with Lieutenant Strang, he is very pleased with their progress and compliments them. He seems to be very busy organising something and has lots of meetings with important-looking people. He also has some more academics study Ehrlichmann's journal and tells them the next part of the artifact is likely in Delhi. Equipping Garance with an experimental weapon, he sends them on the next flight to Calcutta, where they will then board a smaller plane onwards.

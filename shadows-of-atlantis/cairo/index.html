Cairo

Act One: Reflections

According to Ehrlichmann's notes, the original stone is in the Cairo Museum of Antiquities. He also suggests that a local retired Army Engineer, a Major Aloysius Blythe-Manders is worth talking to. After asking around at some bars frequented by military types, they are directed to his home. They are met at the door of the compound by a small man with stunningly bright blue eyes. He takes them to the Major, who welcomes them and offers them food and drinks. He shows them some of his collection of historical artifacts, which he is very proud of. When Garance mentions the museum, the Major offers to write them a letter of introduction.

Meanwhile, Calvin is exploring the garden, where he sees the small man - Sheik Janin El-Rauf - digging. He is obviously taking great pains to show the tattoo on his wrist of a spiral pattern. He speaks to Calvin in a mix of Arabic, English and French. It's clear he wants the agents to come back at night with the flowers of the blue lotus.

At the markets, they buy some of the blue lotus flowers then move on to the museum. They are met by M. Lucien Kutay who tells them about his work updating the museum catalogue. He hasn't seen anything matching the description of the black stone, but he directs them to the card index from just after the Great War. After a bit of searching, they find a card that seems to reference what they are looking for. A bit more persuasion is needed to convince him to let them into the storeroom, but eventually he relents and he accompanies them there. They find the crate with the stone in, and take a fresh rubbing, noticing the same spiral pattern overlaying the text.

That night, the agents head back to the Major's property - but are accosted by street thugs on the way, who they swiftly overcome. At the property, the Sheik lets them in and motions for them to be silent. He removes the cover from the well with Calvin's help, and he explains that the power of the sun has entered the Duat (underworld) and lies there waiting for them to free it. They must find a doorway to the land of the night, and the Well of Stars can grant a vision to help. Louis takes an ostrich feather from him and draws the spiral pattern in the well as the Sheik chants in the background.

In the well they see a sandstone facade supported by six pillars; each of the pillars contains the carved head of a woman with cow's ears: the goddess Hathor. The ruins to the right of the temple split and the sun begins to rise through the crack.

The Sheik takes back his feather, and covers the well. He also lets them know in broken Arabic, English and French:

  • The temple they saw is near Qena on the opposite bank of the Nile.
  • The ruin was the Sanatorium, where the sick would come to find cures through prophetic dreams induced by narcotic beer
  • The agents must make their own journey into the Duat and face the challenges - if their hearts are found to be in balance and they defeat the dread Apophis, the sun will be theirs

He volunteers to make the "red ale of truth" for them, but tells them to come back and collect it in the morning. When they collect it, he says "Keep your metau sharp, and do not be chained by thinking that willow is only of use for making cricket bats."

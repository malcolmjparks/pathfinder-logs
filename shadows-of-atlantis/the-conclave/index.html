The Conclave

Scene 1: Meeting Karl

Karl goes into detail about the party: - About 20 guests at the party - mostly academics and men high up in industry, people who find history interesting or want to hobnob with intellectuals - There was a ritual - started as a joke. This one was led by a women Karl didn't know, but Manfred Stern performed the actual reading of the incantation etc. - He remembers the candles and the chalk circle, but then he is suddenly outside with no coat and was very disturbed by this - Other members of the party reacted similarly - He names a few other people that he knows the addresses of and volunteers to introduce the agents to them

Scene 2: The Investigation

The agents go to visit Frau Dr. Elisabeth Vogt, who has also been having nightmares but is less affected by the compulsions as she was halfway out the room when her memories jumped. She tells them that she remembered the woman who led the ritual did not seem to move and was taking notes. She also thinks that Stern put something on the ground, but maybe picked it up afterwards? Louis shows her the symbol from Piotr's drawing, and she doesn't quite recognise it but suggests it is similar to something she read about that was found in Greenland.

Going to visit Stern at the city archives, they find him blankly performing routine work. He has no memory of going to the villa last night, though his face goes oddly blank when it is mentioned. When Garance mentions the ritual, he opens and then closes a drawer in his desk. Garance convinces him to open it and then lets them take his notebook. He seems confused as to why he let the agents do this, and Garance distracts him while they go over the book. - It contains notes on how to summon and bind a Haunter of the Dark, an entity that steals people's memories - The last three pages are torn out - There is a counter-ritual to banish the Haunter, but it is crossed out. Calvin manages to interpret what was underneath. When Stern returns, he talks about how he has worked on the ritual over the past month or so after he found a valuable tome in a selection of otherwise meaningless books. By happy chance, a fellow enthusiast called Otto Frick contacted him and they have been working together. He gives the agents Frick's address.

Turning up at Frick's address, they find it empty and wiped clean. A car drives off as they open the door.

At Herr Gustav Perl's house, they are met by a man who appears hollowed out. He is welcoming, but keeps smiling all the time and does not blink. He appears to have little memory of anything at all. He also constantly tells the agents that they must come to the party tonight at the villa.

Karl sets the agents up with a tailor to get some nicer clothes for Louis and Calvin. They head to the party fashionably late.

Scene 3: The Villa

When they arrive at the villa, they are welcomed in warmly and shown to the drawing room where Albrecht greets them enthusiastically. They make conversation with him and several other guests, but they all seem slightly off. Calvin talks to one of the wait staff, who tells him that it is unusual to have a party two nights in a row, and that the staff generally keep away from the non-kitchen cellar where the rituals or activities take place. Garance and Calvin sneak away while Louis causes a distraction.

Nearing the cellar, they see a Black Sun operative guarding the passage, but they time their attack to take him out quietly and quickly - with Garance using her garrote and Calvin finishing him off with his tomahawk. They find a note in his pocket - "Holz - if there is any problem at all tonight, ensure Kastner does not make it out alive. Vogel." Calvin dresses up in Holz's uniform to be stealthy.

Heading in to the cellar, they see two figures performing a ritual with a dark shape being contained in the middle of the room. Vera Kastner is confused to see Holz here, and asks him what he is doing. This gives Garance a chance to pull her pineapple grenade and throw it into the room - this has the unfortunate effect of stunning nearly everyone in the room. Kastner orders Frick to continue the ritual and casts a defensive spell. Calvin starts trying to shoot Frick but finds it difficult to damage him.

Louis hears the explosion and the gunshots. The other guests seem to presume that there are some fireworks nearby. He tries to move towards the cellar but the other guests block him from leaving. Meanwhile, Calvin knocks Frick down and he falls unconscious after having his nose chopped off. Kastner shouts "You fools, now it will escape!". She sees Garance trying to cast the counter-spell and reluctantly joins in so they they are not all killed. The Haunter is now actively blowing out candles to try to make the room darker.

Louis uses his dog as a distraction and manages to escape the room - on the way to the cellar, he sees something that could be cobbled together to make a powerful electric lamp which he takes with him. He arrives just as the Haunter extinguishes two of Calvin's flares, and between the four of them they banish the Haunter. With an eerie noise, the dark shape shrinks and vanishes before their eyes.

Kastner goes to treat Frick and tries to sneakily pick something up off the floor. She turns to the agents and seems about to say something but Garance tells her "I think you should look at this" and gives her the note from Vogel. She seems put out by this.

Scene 4: The Negotiation

Garance begins trying to negotiate with Kastner to tell them about the ritual so that she can leave. Kastner smiles and says "I think we can do better than that. I think it is clear my time with Black Sun has come to a close. I suggest my knowledge would be useful to Section D - I assume that is who sent you? I thought so. Let me offer you this - If you get me out of the country and somewhere where I can research properly, I will give you information about three Black Sun cells here in Vienna. Not only that, there is a handoff happening tomorrow night. I will give you what you need to intercept it - I believe this will set back their operations by some time. But only if Frick is treated as a witness and not a prisoner - and if I go with you." She appears to be hiding something but is probably not lying. The agents agree but decide to keep a close watch on her.

Above them, the guests at the party are confused as to why they are there, but are now leaving. Karl seems to be back to normal and thanks the agents profusely. They take Kastner to a secure location and then make contact with Deadman who eagerly confirms that the details of the handoff match their partial intel, and is excited for the agents to grab whatever package is being handed off. He warns them however that Kastner's knowledge is much more valuable.

Scene 5: The Warehouse

The handoff is at a dead drop at 11pm at a warehouse. The agents including Dr. Ralph Bobbins arrive two hours early and spend time finding good hiding spots. Louis hides their car under a tarp. Before 11, a car pulls up outside, and four Black Sun troopers get out. Two remain outside on guard and two come inside - they have a brief look around but do not spot anything unusual. At 11, two more cars drive up - including the staff car with Vogel in the back. The courier steps out of his car with a briefcase, and Vogel's driver gets out and opens Vogels door for him to step out as well. Kastner is in the car with Louis and Ralph, and she obviously stiffens when she sees Vogel.

The exchange is underway, but then a shot rings out - Calvin has fired his rifle right through Vogel's leg, who falls over. A car's engine roars into life, and Louis rams the Black Sun agents, who are scattered and injured. Ralph finishes one off with a shotgun fired out of the passenger window. Kastner leaps from the car and casts a spell as she walks calmly towards Vogel. His driver is trying to drag him back into the car, but is killed by a second shot from Calvin. The other driver panics and drives off. The two Black Sun troopers from outside burst in firing wildly, but Louis runs them over as well (though the engine is shot in the process).

Kastner has made it to Vogel and grabs his pistol - she kneels down to make sure he sees that it is her, and shoots him between the eyes. Standing up, she looks around and sees that there is only one injured Black Sun trooper left. She calmly dispatches him too. She sighs slightly and says to the agents "Well, that went well I think. Now, let's get out of here before all of Black Sun descends on us."

Back at the safehouse, Deadman interviews Kastner about her knowledge (both esoteric and about Black Sun operation) and seems very pleased with the results. He takes the briefcase and looks inside - he quickly shuts it and will not talk about what was inside.

Tomorrow is their meeting with Gisela. It should be interesting.

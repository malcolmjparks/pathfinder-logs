The Last Train

Scene 1: The Handoff

Section D agent Garance de Winter has prepared documents and travel arrangements for herself, her driver Louis Lafite, and an another agent Calvin Crowfoot to travel to Vienna, where they are to meet up with Gisela Waltrun, a German intelligence officer who has unexpectedly contacted Section D asking to meet. Their handler Lieutenant Strang has instructed Garance to also transport a "package" of Piotr Wierzbicki and has arranged cover as "importers of machine parts" on a cargo train.

A woman meets the agents at the station with Piotr, and seems slightly fearful of him. She hands him a small tin with and instruction of not to open, and then abruptly walks away. Garance takes Piotr under her wing and they settle themselves in on the train. Piotr is very taken with Louis' unusual dog, and the dog is also very interested in Piotr. Their container has some seating and small curtained off area for Piotr to sleep.

As they travel, Piotr chats away merrily about his hobbies and his recent past. The agents note that he has a gap in his memory from about three weeks ago, where his father suddenly went missing. Since then, everyone has been telling him that things will be fine and he will see his father again soon. He repeatedly asks whether he can draw. When he starts drawing, he draws intricate vistas of strange alien architecture, with a recurring symbol. Calvin notices that the pictures he draws can be lined up next to each other to form a vista of a great city. Louis tries to take a close look at one of the drawings and accidentally drops it out a window. Piotr is furious.

Scene 2: Border Crossing

The initial journey is uneventful, and everyone sleeps reasonable well. At the border to Czechoslovakia the following morning, a customs patrol boards the train and makes their way through. When they reach the compartment with the agents, the officers are mostly indifferent, but one seems to take umbrage with Calvin and looks at his papers with more detail. He seems like he might raise an alarm, but Garance manages to charm him and they moves on. Piotr has been sitting still with his eyes closed for the whole time of the inspection, but when the train starts moving again he suddenly announces that "The man in the black coat already knows we are on the train."

The agents are on edge, but nothing happens for some time. Then, after passing Bratislava, the lights suddenly go off in a tunnel. After ten seconds the lights come back on, but there is a symbol drawn in the condensation in the glass - the same as that in Piotr's drawings. Piotr appears to be asleep.

Scene 3: The Search

The train starts slowing down unexpectedly. The agents read the signal flags and determine that the train has been instructed to stop for a military inspection. They see some Nazi soldiers in black coats emerge from a troop carrier and head towards the back of the train, and a soldier and a commander head to the front. After a minimal delay, the train starts moving again. The agents have about fifteen minutes before the solders discover them assuming they are searching the train thoroughly. The agents notice that the soldiers are all wearing an armband with a strange symbol.

Calvin heads up front and tries to overcome the two Nazis there. Louis heads back and tries to disengage the rear coaches so the soldiers are left behind. Louis is initially confused about how to disconnect the carriages, but then starts making progress. Calvin tries to ambush the soldiers in the engine, but does not manage to make a killing blow. Garance is staying with Piotr who is drawing a scene of a fight occurring in a train engine.

Calvin takes out the lower-rank soldier - then the commander casts a spell at him, and is shocked when it fails utterly. The commander shoots, but his gun jams - then when attacking with his sword he is shot in the eye and then through the head. Meanwhile, Louis has almost managed to extract the pin that connects the carriages, but a soldier appears and shoots him at the last minute - but it's too late, and the rear coaches fall away as the train lurches forward.

The train driver warns Calvin that the Nazis are setting up a blockage ahead, and that he will slow down at a corner ahead so they can jump off undetected.

Scene 4: The Road to Vienna

The agents and Piotr jump off the train as it slows. Calvin and Louis agree on the best way forward, and promptly lead everyone into a ditch. Eventually, they make their way to a road and after a forty minute walk they find Karl Hoffmann standing over his Opel Kapitan, trying to "fix" the engine by prodding it with various tools. Louis takes matters into his own hands and fixes the engine. Karl is delighted, and invites the agents to accompany him back to Vienna. He invites Louis to drive. He talks all the time about his business, his relations, the car, and many other topics. He noticeably avoids politics, which he finds distasteful. He mentions a party he attended at Count Erich Albrecht's chateau and he is planning to go to another gathering there tonight. He mentions several of the attendees, and the fact that people share strange objects and perform rituals, which he takes as a joke. Piotr whispers to Garance "He has been in a room with them. He doesn't know."

Scene 5: The Roadblock

Karl directs Louis to take a shortcut, but is put out when they round a corner and find a small queue of vehicles in front of a small single-lane bridge - part of the parapet has collapsed and there is a crack in the approach slab. Amongst the waiting vehicles, a smart Mercedes staff car looks like it has someone important on board. Karl leaps out and starts handing out his business card to everyone present. Calvin clears the debris from the bridge and inspects the pillars. He gives a thumbs up, and the Mercedes zooms forward, obviously in a big rush.

Scene 6: The Handover

In Vienna, Karl drops them at their "hotel" and gives the agents some business cards. The agents make their way to the Section D safe-house, where they meet Professor Richard Deadman who takes charge of Piotr. The agents brief him, and he tells them the following:

  • The staff car was owned by Hauptsturmfuhrer Vogel, a Nazi official in Black Sun.
  • Black Sun is a cult that has aligned itself with the Nazi party, though it predates it. They are obsessed with occult rituals and arcane artifacts.
  • The symbol that Piotr has been drawing has appeared in intercepted Black Sun communiques. It has also appeared in secret communiques of another Nazi faction - the Nachtwolfe.
  • Nachtwolfe are a newer group and seem to favour advanced technology with some unknown power source. They are often at odds with Black Sun as they compete over ancient artifacts.
  • Section D has been trying to find an "in" with Count Albrecht's esoteric parties, so the agents should try to attend if they can and report back.
  • One of the attendees that Karl mentioned - Gotthold Fuchs - is a suspected Nachtwolfe agent.

With two days to go until their meeting with Gisela, the agents contact Karl. He is in a panic and asks to meet in the park. There, he keeps inviting them to the party tonight and then telling them he is inviting them without wanting to. He is also missing some memories and has not slept. The agents decide to investigate.

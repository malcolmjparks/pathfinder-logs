Nepal

Act One: Out of the Blue

On the short flight from Allahabad to Delhi, the plane is surrounded by a glowing fog. The pilots get worried because the plane keeps rising. Louis notices the needles on the instruments are swinging wildly, but that the clock has stopped. The air in the plane is getting colder. Calvin notices strange red lights alongside each wing, which seem to be guiding the plane somehow.

After what feels like hours, the fog clears and the pilots land on a grassy field - it is nighttime, despite it only being a few hours since early morning. The stars also do not look right for this time of year. An old man in red Buddhist robes greets the agents as they emerge from the plane by sticking his tongue out. He tells them his name is Tsering Lama and asks them to follow him where they can eat, drink and rest. They try the yak butter tea brought by a young boy Dawa Rabten , and Ralph suffers from altitude sickness.

The following morning they have a brief walk around the valley accompanied by Dawa after he brings them breakfast, and then meet with Tsering Lama. To their amazement, Tsering Lama tells them that he was a young man in Atlantis who escaped its destruction! He also relates the following:

  • The Palladion was an artifact that was removed from Atlantis before its destruction, and its pieces scattered around the world. It is vital that evil forces do not obtain its powers.
  • He's not sure how he is still alive - maybe if he left the valley his days would be numbered.
  • There are other hidden valleys - but some are not refuges but instead actively seek to bring destruction to the world.
  • One such valley is nearby, and is populated by monks in blue robes who have allied themselves with German soldiers with a strange image of a wolf's claw.
  • The Book of the Machine is a guide to what the pieces of the Palladion do - Tsering Lama thought he had the only copy, but his informants have let him know that the blue monks have a copy and they are communicating to Germany using a radio transmitter as they translate it.

He offers his translation of The Book of the Machine and details of the location of two pieces of the Palladion in exchange for disrupting the blue monks and stopping their communications with Nachtwolfe. The agents agree.

They are all dressed in chuba and given ponies to ride. Tsering Lama also gives them each beads on a leather thong for protection, and a silver phurba to Calvin. After five days travelling they reach Lake Manasarovar. At night, Dawa leads them into the lake, where they find themselves walking back out of the water perfectly dry and surrounded by mountains.

Act Two: Waves and Stanzas

The Veiled Monastery is about three miles to the east. As they travel, the agents come up with a plan. They decide to scramble up the cliff side and detach the supporting cables, causing the antenna to fall thirty metres to the ground and destroy it. As they approach, they see that the outer ring wall of the monastery is made of human skulls facing outwards in wooden frames. The antenna is on the roof of a Thangka wall pressed against the cliff, with the outer wall emerging from it on both sides. In the dim moonlight, they also see a wooden hut near this large building.

Calvin tries to scramble down to where the cables are attached - but he slips and lands with a thump on the top of the Thangka wall. He hears some monks emerging from a trapdoor on top of the wall, and surreptitiously uses a grappling hook to hang over the side. Garance heads to the west side of the monastery ring and tries to open a passage through the wall by prising one of the panels free. Louis tries to climb to the cable, and he also slips and falls to the top of the Thangka wall, emitting a loud swearword as he lands.

Louis spies cables going from the antenna to the wooden shack, and surmises that the hut probably houses the transmitter and receiver. Ralph decides this is the right moment to cause a distraction, and throws a grenade to the east - trying to get it over the ring wall, it instead bounces off the wall and falls inside the monastery. With their presence now known, Calvin jams the trapdoor and sets up the grappling hook to be on the side near the wooden hut, lowering Louis down.

Louis lobs a grenade into the hut and retreats. Calvin is still on the top of the wall as a group of monks materialises behind him - seeing this, he also quickly descends, followed by most of the monks who are shouting and raising the alarm...

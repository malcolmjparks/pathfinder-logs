Rome

Act One: New Arrivals

Arriving at Rome, the agents buy a guidebook and head for the city's famous talking statues. At Il Babuino they spot an oak leaf partially covered by some coins in the fountain there. Garance sits and paddles in the fountain for a minute or two, and surreptitiously retrieves it. Taking it to a cafe, they spot that it has a series of little holes pricked in it. They decipher the message as "What lies behind the truth that bites?"

Consulting the guidebook leads them to the Bocca della Verita at the church of Santa Maria, which has a legend associated with it - anyone who puts their hand in the mouth and tells a lie has their hand bitten off. At the church, they have a look round and discover another note - "P taken to Regina Coeli, moved to CSant'A road crew. Archaeologists present appear to be looking for something. Germans have arrived - strange badge on uniform. Bodies from the site taken to Tiber Island hospital. Situation escalating. Sent for help."

At a quick visit to the Section D safe-house at an art supplies shop they discover it has been boarded up and notices from OVRA covering the front, declaring it a haven for political undesirables. Beating a hasty retreat, they are confronted by a filthy raving beggar. He grabs Garance and starts rambling about his "message", "tunnels", "snakes," and "mouth". Ralph manages to calm him down, and they find out he is the agent with the codename Pasquino. He tells them

  • He was arrested by OVRA
  • He was assigned to a gang of labourers working on the road construction, but really they were sent to clear tunnels underneath
  • He knows the way into the labyrinth where the snakes are

At this point he claims "I'd give my right hand to get away from them", pulls out a knife and starts hacking at his wrist with it. Calvin disarms him and subdues him.

Act Two: Snakes & Labyrinths

Scouting the dig-site, they notice it is fenced off, which is unusual. It is guarded by several Blackshirts and German Wehrmacht soldiers - who have an armband with the logo they recognise from Fuchs' notes as belonging to Nachtwolfe. Peeping through gaps in the fence, the agents notice two people having an argument in Latin - they get the impression that there are serious problems in a tunnel system under the site, caused by "snake issues". A truck enters the site and they spot it being loaded with two bodies covered in sheets - though the bodies look not quite right. A workman tries to flee at this point, but is shot dead by the guards.

Deciding to enter the tunnels via the passage that Pasquino used to escape, they take him to the hospital. There, they see the truck that they saw at the dig site, along with two guards who are emerging from the hospital and looking rattled. Dropping Pasquino off, they take this opportunity to scout the hospital. They find a locked door with bolts on this side - they pick the lock and Garance and Calvin sneak down the steps. In a storeroom just before the main cellar, they find some medical coats. Garance puts one on and pretends to be a doctor to find out what is going on. She spots that four of the six bodies are scaled and sinuous, with a serpent head, a stunted tail and clawed feet. All four have a hand missing, but it looks like an old injury. The two human bodies had bite and claw marks. There is also some blood under some microscopes - the "Subject S1" blood looks much more similar to serpent blood than human blood.

As they make to leave, they are confronted at the bottom of the steps by a guard who demands to see their papers. Calvin quickly punches him in the throat, and Garance calls for help in the room and announces that they are going to get help from upstairs. They meet up with Ralph and Louis and make a quick exit.

Act Three: The Truth Will Out

Heading down into the sewers, the agents find the crack that Pasquino used to escape the tunnels and squeeze through. They follow his footprints backwards and explore - eventually finding a chamber with carvings on the wall. They are attacked by Serpent People, but manage to fight them off. Opening the door in the chamber, they find a grotto with a lake at the centre. The walls of the chamber are covered with strange patterns which turn out to be made from finger bones of something inhuman. At the centre of the lake is something that appears to be a fountain. Four alien "dolphins" are supporting a massive upright clam shell with their tails. One side of the clam has a face carved - identical to the Bocca della Verita.

Calvin selflessly puts his hand in the mouth, and he feels a sharp pain - but he can pull his hand out where he sees a small pin-prick in his palm. The clam opens up, revealing a floating golden crystal surrounded with a red metal and glass mount. Making their way out of the tunnels using the same path as they entered, the agents sneak off with the Clypeus and arrange passage to Cairo - held by the British and also another location in Ehrlichmann's journal.

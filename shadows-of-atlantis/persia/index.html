    Persia

    Act One: Heat & Dust

    Arriving in Isfahan, the agents know they are looking for the Cup of Yima nearby, and the local Zoroastrian community may know more. They notice that there is a feeling of unease in town - listening in to a few conversations at the teahouse gives them the information that a growing number of mutilated corpses are being discovered along the river. The bodies have been slashed and their eyes eaten. The locals also mention that whatever creature is doing this only appeared after the French archaeologists and their German engineers turned up, though it is only attacking locals.

    Their contact Kourush Spahany meets them at his stall in the market. He puts them in contact with his brother Jamshid Spahany, a priest in training in the Zoroastrian community. When the agents tell him what they are after, he discusses with them a legend that there is something valuable under the hill where the archaeologists are digging - but it is protected by a guardian. If the agents can stop this guardian and the attacks on the locals, he can help get them into the underground city. They will need to perform a ritual using the fires of victory to see the hidden gateway.

    Act Two: Catching the Wind

    The agents and Jamshid drive to Yazd, where the fires of victory were lit almost 1500 years ago. As Jamshid is entering the temple, Garance notices a boy watching them. As she approaches, he sprints off into the crowd, but between her and Ralph, they track him down to find him talking to a man in a red felt hat. When confronted and threatened, he reveals that he was paid by the French archaeologist to ambush the team on their way back. Given how little they were paid, he is perfectly fine with Garance paying him double to leave them alone instead.

    Jamshid emerges with the embers and some spare ash in case of emergency, and they drive back to Isfahan. With evening falling, they decide to wait for darkness to make their move.

    Act Three: The Purifying Flame

    Creeping up the hillside in the dark, the agents follow Jamshid to the best spot for the ritual. He doesn't stay, as he wants plausible deniability. Ralph performs the ritual, though with some cost to his mental health. The agents all spread ash on their eyelids. With the ritual complete, they see fire emanate from the Tower of Sacrifice. There is a ring of hot flame which they can pass through, and then a doorway made of a cold blue flame. Stepping through, they enter a tunnel underground. Behind them, they see the French and Germans appearing along with a monk in blue robes, but they seem unable to see the agents.

    As they progress, they hear something tracking them. Eventually it comes into view - a huge creature with a lion's head and claws, broad angelic wings, and a man's body etched with writhing symbols. It appears to be blind and is tilting its head from side to side to listen for its prey. After a few shots are fired, it attacks the agents, doing considerable damage before a grenade finishes it off. It disappears with a pop.

    At the centre of the city, they find a chamber holding seven different cups. Garance chooses the correct one and tries to use it. She can't see places far away, but as she uses it to view the Tower of Sacrifice, she is confronted with the sight of the lead French archaeologist brutally killing his colleagues and eating their eyes. They quickly come up with a plan to attack him from the other side of the gateway. With this, they manage to kill the possessed man, finally defeating the aeorimanius for good.

    Searching the dig site reveals a winged and clawed disguise in the lead archaeologist's tent - leaving this for the police to wrap up, they take the cup and head back to England.

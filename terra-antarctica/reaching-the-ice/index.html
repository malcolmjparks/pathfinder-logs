Reaching the Ice

Scene 1: Job Interview

Lucrecia received a telegram from Professor Dyer inviting her to come on his rescue expedition to Antarctica as a pilot. She eagerly agrees, and makes her way to Providence RI to join the crew. At the station, she is met by Jurgen Harkson, who greets her eagerly and drives her to the warehouse at the harbor. As she is crossing the yard and admiring the planes, they are met by Dean who seems to have joined the team due to his uncanny knack of improving engines in a way that defies science.

Making their way to Dyer's office, they meet three more people who have just been interviewed and are joining the expedition - Leroy, Jose and Brock. In his office, they are also joined by Lynn Pabodie who is second-in-command and chief mechanic. They find out about the length of the trip and how much they will be paid. They meet some of the other members of the crew as they start their training.

  • Leroy ends up befriending Lynn and talking about her engineering work at the university
  • Jose bonds with Acacia Drakos over prior mercenary work
  • Brock spends time with the ship's bosun Nguyen Ly where they discuss their former lives of crime
  • Dean eagerly follows the explosives expert Marcus Blaine around - and is getting the uncanny feeling that this is a lot of explosives to be taking on a rescue mission
  • Lucrecia ends up slightly enamored with the chief pilot Manuel Pereira

Scene 2: The Society of Antediluvian Research

Marcus, Acacia and two other military experts (Nico Argyros and Maria Kastallanos) invite the investigators to the Biltmore, a hotel that is openly a speakeasy. While drinking at a table, they are approached by Ms. Neiman, who introduces herself as a representative of the Society of Antediluvian Research. She offers everyone a two-month job for $500 each, which after some discussion the team refuse - after determining that the society almost never publishes academic papers or sponsors public research, and that Ms. Neiman is probably hiding something. They fail to convince Maria however, who decides to take her up on the offer.

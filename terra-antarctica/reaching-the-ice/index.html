    Reaching the Ice

    Scene 1: Job Interview

    Lucrecia received a telegram from Professor Dyer inviting her to come on his rescue expedition to Antarctica as a pilot. She eagerly agrees, and makes her way to Providence RI to join the crew. At the station, she is met by Jurgen Harkson, who greets her eagerly and drives her to the warehouse at the harbor. As she is crossing the yard and admiring the planes, they are met by Dean who seems to have joined the team due to his uncanny knack of improving engines in a way that defies science.

    Making their way to Dyer's office, they meet three more people who have just been interviewed and are joining the expedition - Leroy, Jose and Brock. In his office, they are also joined by Lynn Pabodie who is second-in-command and chief mechanic. They find out about the length of the trip and how much they will be paid. They meet some of the other members of the crew as they start their training.

    • Leroy ends up befriending Lynn and talking about her engineering work at the university
    • Jose bonds with Acacia Drakos over prior mercenary work
    • Brock spends time with the ship's bosun Nguyen Ly where they discuss their former lives of crime
    • Dean follows the explosives expert Marcus Blaine around - and is getting the uncanny feeling that this is a lot of explosives to be taking on a rescue mission
    • Lucrecia ends up slightly enamored with the chief pilot Manuel Pereira

    Scene 2: The Society of Antediluvian Research

    Marcus, Acacia and two other military experts (Nico Argyros and Maria Kastallanos) invite the investigators to the Biltmore, a hotel that is openly a speakeasy. While drinking at a table, they are approached by Ms. Neiman, who introduces herself as a representative of the Society of Antediluvian Research. She offers everyone a two-month job for $500 each, which after some discussion the team refuse - after determining that the society almost never publishes academic papers or sponsors public research, and that Ms. Neiman is probably hiding something. They fail to convince Maria however, who decides to take her up on the offer.

    Scene 3: Danforth Returns

    Leroy is struggling to find his sea-legs, despite still being on land, so takes some time off. Meanwhile, after a meeting with the captain, Lucrecia and Brock notice a suspicious figure moving about the warehouse. Brock sneaks up and recognises Danforth, a former student of Dyer. They find bombs in his bag, and forcibly subdue him - handing him over to Dyer, the two of them have a heated argument about the safety of the expedition. Dyer sadly hands him over to the police, who decide to commit Danforth to the sanitarium. Unfortunately Danforth had already set a bomb, which goes off that night and destroys some of the food and some of the cyclonite charges.

    The ship - the Aurora - sets off with the investigators onboard, and they make a stop at Havana to pick up rum and cigars, then everyone undergoes a ceremony as they cross the equator - led by Bosun Nguyen and aided by Brock. Manuel expresses distrust of some members of the crew.

    Scene 4: Mount Erebus

    Arriving at Ross Island just off Antarctica, the crew and expedition start unpacking everything. After talking to Dyer, the investigators (minus Leroy) head out up to the top of Mount Erebus to see if they can see anything of the Delaware. Lucrecia struggles with the climb and injures herself. They spot the ship about 20 miles away, and decide to head back to the Aurora and take a motor launch there the next day.

    Scene 5: Wreck of the Delaware

    Arriving at the ship, they notice that it is listing and has taken on water. They also find several crates and a slightly damaged Hercules airplane lashed to the ice. Entering the ship, they find body parts and blood everywhere, as if something monstrous ripped the crew apart. Exploring further, they find the bodies of two crew members in the propellor shaft, with strange symbols carved into the bulkheads next to them.

    Scene 6: Tek-tek Horror

    The team is attacked by three Tek-Teks, which slash at them, injuring Lucrecia and causing some horror for everyone. As they are killed, they drop fragments of a stone tablet. Once the Tek-Teks are all killed, the investigators hear Tek-Tek noises coming from other parts of the ship. Jose decides to hunt them down, and once they have destroyed four more, they have all the pieces of the tablet.

    Putting it together and reading the inscription aloud, the tablet seals itself together and the black swirling mist around the pieces disappears. The team decide to throw the tablet into the Antarctic Ocean to avoid summoning more Tek-Teks.

    Scene 7: Reporting Back

    The team arrive back at the Aurora, and report their finding to Dyer, who is with Lynn and Jurgen. They all seem quite unfazed, almost as if they had been expecting something like this. Manuel reiterates their suspicions of a few of those aboard the Aurora.

    Scene 8: Finding the Starkweather-Moore Camp

    It takes a day to set up the planes. Dean heads off to try to repair the Hercules by the Delaware. Lucrecia pilots one of the planes, with the others helping to navigate. The first search is unsuccessful, and the team have to head back to the Aurora to refuel. On the second attempt, they spot the camp and land to explore.

    Scene 9: Exploring the Camp

    The camp looks like it has been attacked - a Hercules plane has its wing melted and bodies lie with limbs ripped off. Brock finds an expedition journal. Leroy and Jose think that between 10 and 20 bodies are unaccounted for. They also think that the deaths are very strange, with flesh being melted away leaving bone behind.

    Scene 10: Conference

    Captain Khatri wants to leave and head back home, but Dyer insists that they press on. The team help to convince everyone to stay and help.

    Scene 11: Sabotage!

    With everyone suspicious, the team decide to perform guard duty overnight. Brock and Jose take the first shift, which is quiet. Lucrecia and Leroy take the second, and as they are patrolling they see one of the planes go up in flames! Two of the member of the expedition and three members of the crew have petrol bombs and pistols! A fight breaks out, and a second plane is set alight - luckily Lucrecia manages to put out the flames before it is too damaged, but the first plane is destroyed. Brock and Jose emerge from the tents to help with the fight, and they kill most of the cultists. One last cultist is restrained and he is eventually intimidated into saying that the Society of Antediluvian Research is behind the attacks.

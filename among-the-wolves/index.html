    Among the Wolves

    It's July 1940, less than two weeks since the Battle of France. The remnants of the French Army and the British Expeditionary Force have retreated to Dunkirk, with little hope remaining.

    Characters

    Name Archetype Background About Portrait
    James Mason Infiltrator Navy Raised in the colonies, James became an undercover agent after his stint serving on submarines. He has not disclosed his Lieutenant rank and is posing as a Private.
    Yuri Molotov Soldier Resistance In the resistance, Yuri was well known for two things - the fact that he was built like a brick outhouse, and for always getting the job done. He's now bringing his expertise to the Allies' effort in France.
    Eva Mettershed Boffin Academic Eva was a Doctor of Psychology, but discovered another side to the world after walking in the Dreamlands. When she returned, she brought a pet koala with her, who has the knack of seeing things that people don't.
    Ciaron Dixon Commander Army The numbers tattooed on Cieron's arm remind him of his past as an experimental subject, where he was injected with experimental drugs to increase his strength. Since the experiment finished with mixed results, he has made a new life in the army as a commando.
    JJ Laurent Grease Monkey Covert Operative When his organised crime ring was shut down, the covert operative JJ made a new identity to avoid the authorities. His expertise in engineering came in very handy when the war started, and he knows he still has connections that may be able to help him out if needed.

    Act 1: Fight Together, Win Together!

    Scene 1: Hold the Line!

    James, Ciaron and JJ are part of the relief squad dispatched to build and maintain one of the barricades. They meet up with Lieutenant Atterbury, who commands them to shore up the defenses and start repairs on the tank. With the sandbags placed, he is then shot by a sniper and the German Heer forces advance. JJ tries to repair the tank ASAP. James mans the mounted Bren machine gun and starts shooting the German troops, while Ciaron takes position and starts firing with his shotgun.

    Yuri and Eva are walking down the road with orders for the C.O. when they hear shots ahead. Eva takes position behind the barricade, while Yuri uses his resistance background to sneak behind the position of some enemy troops. One of the allied soldiers behind the barricade goes down, but Private Jones keeps up covering fire.

    James manages to beat back some of the troops with sprays of machine gun fire, and eventually JJ repairs the tank to great effect - he decimates two Heer squads, while Yuri takes out one commander from behind, but another soldier turns round just as he is attacking them.

    Eventually, there is a call for a general retreat for the German forces. Private Jones promises to buy them all beers, and Yuri and Eva introduce themselves to the rest of the squad. They pass the orders over to Ciaron, who discovers that the five of them are to report immediately to Lieutenant Archibald Strang.

    Scene 2: New Orders

    Heading back to the beachfront, the squad are met with the site of long lines of troops awaiting rescue. At the makeshift HQ, they find medical staff who patch them up and Strang who motions them over and waves others away. Everyone notices numerous documents marked with "Confidential", "Top Secret" and "Section M". Strang tells them that the German troops appear to be holding back, though some mysterious secondary agency is taking their place. They are to gather information behind enemy lines, and then meet up with Professor Richard Deadman, codename Arrow at the Church of St. Marie and speak a code phrase to him. They are then directed to Captain Appleby - the quarter-master - to pick up some gear.

    After being kitted out, Appleby sternly warns that she wants every round not used to drop a Jerry to be returned, and she is vocal about her lack of faith in the squad's abilities. Eva retorts, and Appleby is impressed by her response. She decides to give the team an experimental BB gun that can fire poisoned pellets, the Whisperer, though she wants a full report upon its return.

    Scene 3: A New Threat

    Making their way East, past enemy lines, the squad try to pick up their enemies' trail. They are successful in the task, and manage to avoid detection from the patrolling Germans, though they lost a couple of guns when escaping quickly from detection. Eventually they hear idling engines coming from a town square, and make their way to a vantage point where they spot some Heer soldiers and a large number of soldiers dressed in black greatcoats adorned with a red armband. Two figures are conversing in the centre of the square.

    Scene 4: Square of Slaughter

    The team notice that the black-clad troops outnumber the Heer troops by about three to one. The black-clad troops wear metal masks - not gas-masks, more like theatrical masks. They also see a hidden machine-gun nest behind the Heer troops. Watching the scene with binoculars, they are surprised when one of the men in the centre bellows "We are soldiers, not treasure hunters! Whatever madness you and your insane Schwartz Sonne ilk follow is no concern of mine not of the Reich's."

    As the man walks away, a Luger fires and he falls dead. The Black Sun troops in the machine gun nest fires on the Heer troops, joined by the Panzer. The Heer are cut down in moments. The other Black Sun troops then go and finish off any survivors with pistols.

    With the Black Sun troops ordered to search the area for stragglers, the squad sneak away.

    Act 2: In the Wolves' Den

    Scene 1: Bad News, Dangerous Plans

    The squad find a way to the rendezvous site, where they meet up with Deadman. They tell him about the scene at the square, and he looks very interested. He tells them that Black Sun are sorcerers, and that the squad should 'liberate' some uniforms, infiltrate their encampment and do what they can to stop them.

    Scene 2: Sheep in Wolves Clothing

    With a healthy amount of teamwork, the squad manage to target a squad of Black Sun Troopers, and bottleneck them in a narrow alleyway. With a surprise attack, they start to overcome the trapped troops, but backup appears with some additional troops carrying some additional firepower - including a flamethrower! Yuri manages to wrestle the holder in such a way that he catches the Trooper on fire, killing him. The Whisperer does a good job in killing troopers over time, and soon they kill all the enemy and dress up in their uniforms (remembering to wear a gold armband from Deadman under the Black Sun armband).

    Scene 3: Among the Wolves

    Making their way back to the square, they see some of the dead Heer bodies being loaded into flatbed trucks. An Officer approaches and barks orders to prepare the Opel Blitz for use. They see it has taken a few stray bullets, but JJ fixes it easily enough. As he finishes, a tall blonde engineer approaches and compliments JJ in German with a slight Austrian accent. He introduces himself as Ulbrecht Schroder.

    General orders to board the trucks are issued, and everyone gets aboard. On the journey, the Troopers are silent and unmoving, but the Engineers joke with one another. The journey ends overlooking a small farm, and work starts on setting up camp.

    Scene 4: The Wolf's Lair

    Schroder pulls the squad aside and tells them that they have been reassigned to him for the remainder of the operation, and leads them to a tent where they can rest.

    Eva's koala leads her over to the officer's tents, where she sees two Black Sun leaders - Ranulf Lange and Jan Stöller - attempting some form of ritual over a body from the square, but is disturbed before she can find out what.

    Yuri sees an engineer working on a selection of firearms. He offers to help, and after an hour or so he notices a strange blue glow emanating from a hidden crate. He opens it and finds a selection of unusual weapons, a large partially-assembled sniper rifle beside a selection of blue-tipped rounds and several unusual belt-fed shotguns. He also finds a note with the symbol of the Nachtwölfe.

    JJ is approached by Schroder, who asks for help in tuning the engine of the "Grendel" - a heavily modified tank fitted with a drill. Schroder slips into French when chatting, but JJ answers in German.

    James walks around the camp and observes troop movement. He finds out

    • Some Black Sun troopers are heavily scarred and deformed beneath their masks
    • A fully armed squad has been sent down the hill to the nearby farms
    • Troopers obey orders without question. They seems to lack free will
    • The Black sun seems to be hunting for details on a subterranean cave system, as well as a large, insect-like creature

    Ciaron hears a shout of pain from outside, and going outside finds an injured engineer, who introduces himself as Wolfram Koch. Cieron helps patch him up, but doesn't notice anything untoward.

    Before settling down for the night, the squad "borrow" a Stielhandgrenate.

    Scene 5: Breakout

    The squad is woken early by a loud voice before the tent is thrown open by Schroder. They are a bit slow getting up and out, raising the suspicion of the Black Sun officers. Stöller orders the squad to take the Grendel to a farm nearby, along with Koch. Eva tries to drive the Grendel, and eventually manages after accidentally flattening one of the tents.

    At the farm, they see four full squads of Black Sun troopers. The Grendel is parked in the barn, and Stöller orders it made ready to drill through the barrier there which leads underground. He stabs a local man and an eerie black mist comes from the wound and flows into Stöller. He then orders you to "bring out the others - start with the soldier".

    Heading in to the house, they find eight civilians and a British soldier, all tied up and watched over by five Black Sun troopers. Using the element of surprise, they take down four of the five troopers without them making a noise, but the last gets a shot fired, which alerts another squad who come to investigate. The manage to convince the squad that all is well and they just had to shoot one of the prisoners. They free and arm the civilians, and bring the soldier Frank Goff out as the bait for a trap.

    With Stöller and a couple of troopers gathered together, they strategically throw their grenade, stunning and damaging them. With the squad now firing on the other Black Sun troopers, Stöller decides to make a break for it and dives into the barn, running to the back and then bursting out on a motorbike which he drives off. With the civilians holed up in the house shooting at the Black Sun Squads near them, and Frank using an MG34 to great effect, the team defeat the remaining Black Sun troops, with JJ backing the Grendel out and using the mounted weapons on that as well.

    Frank thanks the squad for their effort, tells them that "There's something in the caves that Jerry wants, you'd best get down there and nab it first." before escorting the civilians towards Dunkirk. The squad load up into the Grendel, which makes short work of the barrier. They head into the pitch black of the tunnels.

    Act 3: The Deep Blue

    Scene 1: Into the Dark

    Driving the Grendel through the tunnels, the squad encounter bats, flooding, a gas pocket, an underground lake with a giant pair of eyes that only JJ sees, and strange blue crystals. At some point, they hear noises of other engines in pursuit. Eventually, they arrive slightly banged up at a strange portal.

    Scene 2: An Ominous Portal

    A huge stone door lies ahead, with an obelisk on either side. Alien inscriptions seem to refer to creatures that call themselves Mi-Go. James climbs inside one of the obelisks, and sees a Mi-Go plugged in to alien electronics. After a couple of accidents and electric shocks, they disable both obelisks and the doors slide open. They decide to sneak in quietly.

    Scene 3: The Impossible Pyramid

    Ahead they see latticed buildings, some of which are suspended in the air. Hundreds of insectoid Mi-Go flit from place to place. A giant pyramid hangs in place in front of them, aglow with a vivid blue light. A gigantic metal barrel points away from them. Yuri guesses it is a giant cannon, probably with enough firepower to flatten a city.

    Making their way stealthily through the city, they get slightly lost but eventually manage to reach the base of the pyramid without being noticed. Then, the sound of crumbling rock and a massive salvo of cannon fire erupts, as the Black Sun arrive. The colony erupts into all-out war between the Black Sun and the Mi-Go.

    Scene 4: The Ascent

    At the base of the pyramid, the squad find s cohort of four Mi-Go and a dangerous Augmented Mi-Go, armed with a disintegrator. A long battle takes place, with several agents going down injured. Eventually they prevail, and take the lift up into the pyramid itself. There, they meet a Mi-Go Scientist, who attacks them with an electric gun - but he is defeated as well. In the lab, they see where the "Pyramid Mega Cannon" is pointed - towards an underwater city filled with strange hideous fish men.

    They determine that the gun is powered by the strange blue crystals they have spotted, and they they can overload and destroy the gun by destroying six of the power regulators.

    Scene 5: Fire and Lightning

    Ascending on a lift to the surface of the pyramid, they find that gravity is lessened, and they can easily jump up the steps. With the battle raging on below them, they start to shoot at and disable the regulators to overload them. Once one is disabled, Mi-Go start flocking towards them, but then Black Sun also turn up.

    Once four regulators have been destroyed, an alien above them falls, consumed by a sorcerous blast. Ranulf Lange, the Black Sun Master steps forward and casts a spell - but the agents resist due to their gold armbands. Then, a gunshot rings out, and Ranulf falls down, revealing Ulbrecht Schroder, now with eyes and muscles glowing blue. The other engineers quickly unload the crate that Yuri spotted, and the Nachtwölfe turn on the Black Sun. Ulbrecht says "Please step aside. We want one of the regulators, and I would rather not kill you should it be avoidable."

    With the Nachtwölfe now forming an orderly retreat, Ulbrecht leaps off the side of the pyramid and blasts his way to one of the Grendels. The Black Sun are in disarray, giving the squad time to disable the final regulators. They quickly retreat to the elevator as the cracks rapidly form along the cannon's length.

    They reach another Grendel with the battle still raging around them, and race off into the tunnels. Racing forward, they are pursued by the Mi-Go, but a few well-placed shots knock them back. Navigating the tunnels, they are fired upon by another Grendel! With some clever steering and some return fire, they break free. With one last push, they emerge from the tunnels, with the entrance collapsing behind them and the tread on their Grendel snapping as they spin through the farm.

    Debrief

    On the way back to Dunkirk, they are met by Frank Boff (along with some reinforcements), who is very pleased to see them. Back at the base, they are met by Strang, Appleby and Deadman. Appleby berates them for losing pistols, but is pleased with their report on the Whisperer, and delighted at the Disintegrator and Electric Gun weapons from the Mi-Go. The agents are all given a small envelope sealed with a wax 'M', inviting them to Clemens Park, where a unique opportunity awaits.

    When they reach there and are debriefed again, Viscount Towton asks them "How do you fancy a trip to Africa?"

    Interlude - Operation Ultimatum

    Before setting off from Dunkirk, James, JJ and Cieron are given orders to quickly head out and see what happened to a missing convoy that includes Section M research operative Professor Paschal.

    Scene 1: Mitigating Losses

    Riding along the expected route of the convoy, the agents quickly find the remains of three trucks, a jeep and a tank. They can immediately tell that the convoy was attacked by Luftwaffe, and there doesn't appear to be any survivors at first. Looking around, they spot the tracks of some survivors heading west into the woods, and they find the Professor's body underneath an overturned truck. JJ finds a note in his pocket, listing the contents of a crate - including four samples of Blauer Kristall and two experimental weapons. After a bit of tracking, they see the marks of the crate being dragged to the south.

    At this point, one of the "bodies" wakes up and starts shooting. JJ convinces her that they are here to help, and they patch her up. James gives her a motorbike so she can make her way to Dunkirk and report.

    Scene 2: The Ruin on the Hill

    Following the drag marks, the agents find themselves at the bottom of a hill. At the top is a ruined castle, but bright blue light arcing like lightning flickers within the bailey. They also spot a trio of German soldiers, which they identify as more Nachtwölfe. JJ convinces them that the agents are undercover operatives sent to investigate the castle, and they are allowed up.

    Scene 3: The Yuggoth Antenna

    Four Mi-Go are flying above the agents, moving between three tangles of wire and metal secured to the inner walls of the curtain wall. The Mi-Go initially ignore the agents, but when they start taking things out of the crate two fly down to attack. Cieron blasts one out of the air with his shotgun, and they quickly equip the experimental weapons in the crate. James climbs up and disables the highest device, and JJ disables the one at ground level. At this point, the walls start to melt. Cieron and JJ shoot another Mi-Go, and then the Nachtwölfe turn up in a truck - however Cieron manages to get the Green Knight Directed Energy Rifle to work, and uses it to cut all of them and their truck in half.

    With the last mechanism only partially disabled, the lightening briefly turns red, orange, yellow and then dissipates - the agents briefly experience strange echoes in their mind. The remaining Mi-Go fly off into the night.

    Debrief

    The goal of these Mi-Go remains a mystery, but the agents are successful in returning the Professor's research, experimental weapons and the remaining Blauer Kristall back to base.

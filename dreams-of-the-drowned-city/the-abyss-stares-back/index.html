The Abyss Stares Back

The Descent Below

After the attack by the Deep Ones, the team decide to back to Kingsport briefly to hire some additional diving equipment. McG drives the boat around in circles before Peter takes the helm. They leave the cargo ship and Eli at the docks, warning him to stay quiet for now.

Back at the location they found using the map, Dan and Peter put the diving suits on and go over the side to have a look around. They see bodies of dead sailors floating in the water, with an unsettling jerk every once in a while. There is an eerie glow coming from some of the stones of an old ruin here, and they also see some crates scattered around. Looking in some of the crates, they find bottles of whiskey and guns, and they start the process to load them onto the boat. Dan also finds a box made of a mysterious substance, and inside a sword engraved with runes.

At that moment, a huge hunting deep one attacks them underwater, and at the same moment four smaller deep ones climb up the side of the boat to attack McG and Keith. Dan uses the sword, and as it cuts into the hunting deep one, the runes glow briefly. He does a lot of damage, and then Peter finishes it off with a harpoon throw to the head. McG and Keith manage to finish off the deep ones on the boat.

All in all, the team recover

  • Three crates of high-end whiskey
  • Two crates of luxury canned goods including caviar
  • One crate of rifles

as well as the sword. Dan takes a look at the glowing stonework, and thinks it is similar to what he saw in Hyperborea - but all he can see is a fallen over doorway half buried in the silt.

Return to Kingsport

The investigators patch themselves up as they head back to Kingsport. The O'Bannion workers hired to collect the goods from the boat unload what there is and drive off in a hurry, saying that they were disturbed to see a couple of people just walking off the docks and into the sea.

On the way back to Arkham, Dan drinks a lot of whiskey to try to settle his nerves - he does a little, but he also ends up drunk.

The Dreaming Ones

At the Clover Club, Seamus is angry that only some of the goods have been recovered, but as McG is giving his report, a waitress suddenly stiffens, her eyes rolling back, and she starts speaking in a deep, rumbling voice — not her own. Then, the walls tremble, and twisted figures of shadow emerge from the corners of the room, clawing toward the investigators.

McG tries to subdue the waitress, seeing that she is scared and that maybe something is possessing her. Failing to do that, he then shoots her. The others attack the shadows, but find that their guns do little damage. As the shadows tough the investigators, they are temporarily dragged into a dream-state. Keith's casting of Azure Flame and Dan's sword make short work of them however, until the enchantment on the sword backfires and hurts Dan. They finish the last shadow - as each of them is destroyed, the investigator fighting them hears a voice in their head say "The Sleeper will know your names."

Seamus appears to no longer trust the investigators and orders them to fix this mess before storming out. The investigators rest and decide to meet at Amos's house in the morning to decide on their next move.

That night they sleep restlessly, and in their dreams they see the green city again — only this time, something is awake within it.

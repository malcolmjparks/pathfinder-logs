The Merging of Worlds

Searching for Survivors

The investigators have to leave their car as they come to Arkham, as the streets are blocked with vehicles that look like they have been abandoned mid-drive. They make their way to Amos's house - as they pass other houses, they see meals left half-eaten on the tables, books dropped on the floor, all signs that whatever made the people disappear, it was sudden.

At Amos's house, they enter and find writing on the wall. McG and Amos manage to decipher some of it, which appears to warn about the fog. At this time, they notice a large bank of fog moving towards the house - and out of the fog they hear the sound of wet footsteps. A group of mindless thralls with bulging eyes and gill-like slits on their throats attack the investigators. The team manage to dispose of them, though at one point McG runs away screaming.

As the fog moves on, they see other thralls mindlessly following in its wake. They decide to follow, and as they approach French Hill, they see a face emerge from a church and beckon them in. Father Mateo introduces himself along with a couple of dozen other survivors. They tell of when the fog rolled in, and suddenly the city was not itself anymore. Strange shapes moved through the streets, and anyone who heard their whispers vanished. A few people resisted the change, but most walked willingly toward the spires, vanishing inside.

They saw figures in robes, performing some kind of ritual at the Miskatonic Museum. The investigators decide to see whether there is anything still there.

At the Museum

Father Mateo comes with them, and they enter the museum without any trouble. It's dark inside - the main lights are not working, but the display lights are providing illumination. As the team split up to investigate, the huge formless blob of a shoggoth drops from the ceiling and starts slithering towards them, eyes and tentacles appearing and disappearing on its outer membrane.

It knocks over Peter and Dan, who desperately fight back against it. McG uses a petrol bomb against it, and breaks his flamethrower attacking it - it is vulnerable to fire, so this does help. Eventually the shoggoth is defeated, and the team can take a look around the museum. Most things look normal, but one particular display looks like it has had a strange wooden idol stolen from it. The description of the idol tells them that it was discovered underwater near Devil Reef, and that it had some strange script on the base. Amos and Keith recognise the symbols as being similar to one particular alien spire that they passed on the way here.

The Green Spires

The team find the spire with the symbols on, and start to make their way up. The passages in the tower seem to deft logic, with windows showing different heights and different angles of the city. Eventually they climb to the top of a spiral staircase and find themselves in a forest clearing with puddles around them. A Deep One Cultist is performing a ritual at an altar, and four Hunting Deep Ones protect it. As the investigators attack, they find that their shots on the cultist are deflected to the hunters. Nevertheless, they manage to take down all the deep ones and stop the ritual.

They find themselves back in the top in a room at the top - looking out over Arkham, they see the city flicker between the normal version, the version with the underwater city and then a few other versions seem to overlap as well!

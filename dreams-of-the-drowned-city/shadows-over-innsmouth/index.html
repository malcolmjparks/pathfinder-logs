    Shadows Over Innsmouth

    Arrival in Innsmouth

    Dan arrives at Amos's house early in the morning and wakes everyone up. Keith makes breakfast while McG and Amos examine the engraved sword that they recovered from the seabed. The runes do indeed look Hyperborean, and in Amos's notes he finds reference to something similar in Innsmouth, though without any details. They take a car to the coastal town, which is only about an hour's drive away - though it is known to be unfriendly to strangers.

    Driving through town, they notice the Marsh Refinery spewing smoke, and park near the Congregational Church. Walking around the area, they notice that they appear to be being followed. The Order of Dagon Hall is by far the nicest building in town, which is otherwise very run down and dilapidated. As they go towards it, they are approached by an old man who warns them cryptically: “The sea remembers. You shouldn’t be here.”

    The front room of the hall has plenty of people coming in and out and using the meeting rooms there, and examination of the pictures on the wall leads to some clues about the Marsh family. There is a guarded entrance to the back which piques the investigators interest - McG causes a brief distraction and Keith then casts Azure Flame to set fire to a corner of the room. With everyone distracted, they slip through the guarded door.

    In the back room, they find themselves amongst cases of artifacts, shelves of old books and tomes and a strange altar. Dan secures a way out by breaking the window latch and opening it up. He also manages to lever open one of the cases, stealing a gold necklace set with black pearls. McG picks open another case to take a similar gold crown, and then helps Amos and Keith to snatch as many books as possible. They then climb out the window and hide in an alley while McG retrieves the car and brings it round - everyone piles in and they head speedily back to Arkham to see what they managed to grab.

    At Amos's house again, they examine the books. Keith is delighted to find the Tome of Oceiros, which has some spells in it. They also find records of the Order of Dagon, which details the deal made with the Deep Ones, where they bred with one of the Marsh family in the past, and their descendants are cursed to turn into Deep Ones as they get older. Part of the town that rebelled 70 years ago was wiped out, and now everyone there is under their influence. The Deep Ones supply gold for the town, which is processed at the Marsh Refinery.

    There is also details of a sunken city out at Devil Reef, and an altar on the coast near Innsmouth that is visible when the tide is low. McG goes shopping and cobbles together a flame-thrower from the pieces he holds.

    The Marsh Refinery

    Heading back at night, the team park near the abandoned railway, and try to sneak into town. At first they are successful, with nobody noticing them as they mirror the unusual shambling gait of the locals. However, just as they approach the refinery, someone shouts a warning. McG quickly picks the lock of the front gate, and they all rush in. Shutting the gate, they find enough empty crates to barricade it and Amos also jams the gate with a metal pole.

    Dan and Amos hang back to keep watch, while McG finds some fireproof overalls. He starts putting them on, as Peter opens the door into the employee rest area - four men with protruding eyes are already standing with improvised weapons ready to throw, but Peter manages to take two of them out with his shotgun right away. Keith casts Kiss of Brine to knock another one over, and between them they finish the last guy off.

    In a storeroom on the other side of the refinery, they find crates of contraband - some belonging to the O'Bannions and some from elsewhere. They also find two smaller crates, which they open to find gold coins and black pearls. They discuss how to fence the goods, but at that moment another huge Hunting Deep One and six smaller Deep Ones enter the refinery from the rear.

    Dan Pacho rushes towards them, and McG tries out his flamethrower to huge success. The Hunting Deep One fails to curse Dan, and falls soon afterwards. The other Deep Ones swipe at Dan, but are taken out without too much bother.

    The investigators find a truck out the rear entrance, which they load up with the crates as quickly as possible, and decide to head back to Arkham before the whole town comes after them. But will they be pursued?

    Black Tides in Kingsport

    The Drowned Men

    Since their return from the battle against Adelei and Ithaqua, the investigators have been plagued by recurring dreams of a vast, impossible city, its green stone spires stretching toward a sky that should not exist.

    Seamus O'Bannion calls them in for a meeting at the Cover Club. He tells them that Kingsport (the town at the mouth of the Miskatonic River) is where the O'Bannions' illicit goods that arrive by ship are unloaded. Recently, a major shipment never arrived - in addition some bodies have washed up on the docks, and their main contact at the lighthouse Silas McBride has fallen silent.

    Arriving in town, the team visit the sheriff's office where they are told by a deputy that the sheriff is at the morgue inspecting the bodies. Keith tries to find a shop that has magical tomes, but only finds a curiosity shop that is filled with unusual preserved animals. The owner is particularly pleased with a recent acquisition of a human-like arm with green scaly skin and webbed fingers.

    At the morgue, McG then introduces themselves as investigators of the paranormal and the sheriff is pleased to see them. He claims that the strange eldritch runes on the bodies are like nothing he's seen before. McG is searching one of the bodies when it suddenly stirs into life.

    The three other bodies rise up as well and start moving towards the investigators. The morgue attendant rushes and hides behind Dan, and the rest of the investigators manage to kill the zombies again before they do too much damage. On the first body, McG finds a map of the area with a mark drawn off the coast.

    The Lighthouse and the Beacon

    At the inn by the docks, the team makes contact with the barman, who is a member of the O'Bannion gang. He tells them that Old Tom can take them out on his boat if they want. After discussion, the investigators decide to go up to the lighthouse first. Arriving by car, they find the lighthouse working but the door hanging off its hinges.

    Upstairs, they find Silas's body frozen in position, arms reaching towards the sea and seaweed stuffed into his mouth. There is also a pulsing crystal that seems to pulse in time with their visions of the green city. The mist outside the window seems to coalesce and a figure drifts through the glass and into the lighthouse room. It reaches out and touches Silas's body, which disappears into the mist leaving no trace behind.

    McG and Peter shoot at the spirit, but don't cause any harm to it. Keith manages to cause some damage with an Azure Flame, but is knocked on the head by a flying object the spirit throws at him. With some aid from the others, Keith manages to finish it off, and it disappears into nothing. Keith takes the pulsing gem with him.

    The Phantom Ship

    Heading down to the docks, the arrange with Old Tom to take a diving suit with them on to the boat. Setting off, they reach the spot marked on the map within an hour - and they find the missing ship adrift in the fog. The investigators climb onboard, noticing that the deck is wet and deserted. They head a rasping voice coming from the captain's cabin, where they find Eli Turner with his eyes a milky white and babbling about "The city... the city wakes..."

    At this moment, Peter warns the team that they are under attack as eight Deep Ones climb onto the deck. The team rushes out to intercept and there is a big battle, with Dan getting hit with a harpoon and Keith at one point running scared back in to the cabin. Eventually all the Deep Ones are killed, and Pete manages to calm Eli down. He tells them "I saw a vision of a temple rising from the sea - then my crew just walked willingly overboard! Something dragged the goods on deck into the water as well. I couldn't understand what they were all saying - but they kept saying over and over... Cthulhu!"

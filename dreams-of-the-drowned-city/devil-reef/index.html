    Devil Reef

    Getting In

    Entering an exposed tidal tunnel, the investigators find passages leading down into the rock. At first they can wade through the thigh-high water in the passages, though a couple of slips show that the way is treacherous. In one chamber, they find old bootlegging gear, at least fifty years old and smelling strongly of brine. They also find a large anemone attached to the ceiling, which drops down to attack but is quickly killed. The noise alerts a group of eight deep ones, who emerge from other tunnels and attack. By confining them to choke points and making good use of their weapons, the team finish these ones off with little fuss.

    In the next chamber, they see pools of water in the floor, and they can see an underwater tunnel leading from one. Dan Pacho decides to risk it, and swims through the tunnel, emerging in a small chamber with a cracked altar. Upon the alter, he spots a gold cloak - and without hesitation he puts it on, and later is pleased to find out he can swim with great ease.

    Creeping round the corner, he spots a large crowd of deep ones (at least twenty plus another three of the large hunting deep ones) chanting "Y'ha Nthlei" over and over towards a stone statue wearing a gold crown. He decides to go back and warn the others.

    The investigators sneak into the chamber and cause a distraction by throwing a petrol bomb at the deep ones. McG lets rip with his flamethrower and Keith shoots Azure Flame everywhere. Dan attacks with his sword, and Peter uses his new machine gun to great effect. Dan does injure his leg though, and decides to disengage from the monsters to pick up the crown and wear it.

    Eventually all the deep ones are defeated, and Dan narrowly misses becoming cursed. McG notices that the tide appears to be rising unnaturally fast, and he suggests making a break for it back the way they came while they still can.

    Getting Out

    The team struggle to wade through the passages, with water everywhere up to their waists now. In the chamber with the bootlegging gear, they are dismayed to see the next tunnel completely underwater. Two of the team manage to quickly fashion barrels to provide some advantage when swimming, and they emerge out into the cave leading out - to see the exit being blocked by another hunting deep one. Though everyone is suffering from injuries, they manage to defeat the final enemy - Dan is interested to see that he appears to be extra strong and quick when he is in water and wearing the crown.

    They swim out to find their boat still there and Old Tom and Amos looking very worried.

    Getting Home

    On the boat, the team take some time to patch themselves up, and Amos helps with healing who he can. At that moment however, they hear an enormous rushing sound and see the city from their dreams emerge from the sea. The green spires stretch up into the sky, but then a fog appears that covers everything. After a few seconds the fog is blown away, and the city is gone.

    Arriving back in Kingsport, the team drive back to Arkham - but when they arrive back, things have changed considerably. Arkham now has alien green spires rising from buildings, there are rivers of fog appearing and disappearing flowing through the streets, and the city appears to be deserted.

Reflections in the Depths

Escaping the Tower

Out of the windows, the investigators see Arkham changed in strange and disturbing ways, with the changes flowing over the city and then disappearing. Another version of Amos suddenly appears in the doorway, saying "Oh my god, you are still alive! You must get out!" before promptly disappearing. McG and Amos lead the way out of the tower to try to find a way down, but when the others turn the corner the two of them have disappeared! Keith leads the way, with Dan, Peter and Father Mateo following. After a few minutes, they bump into future versions of themselves - after an initial bit of confusion they work out what is going on, and future Dan hands present Dan a strange metal rod, which he says might be helpful.

After trying to go down and finding themselves back up high, the remaining investigators find past versions of themselves. Present Dan hands past Dan the strange metal rod, which he says might be helpful. Keith has the brainwave that maybe they need to go up to get down to ground level, and sure enough after a few minutes of going to opposite way to what seems necessary, they find themselves out at street level.

As they walk through the streets, they see things from various times, some of which appear to be in the future - but some a long way in the past. At one intersection they are approached by three growling wolves, but Peter blasts them with his gun and the third is finished off pretty quickly.

The Kelp Club

Trying to decide what to do, the investigators head to a nearby club which is playing strange music the like of which they have not heard before. Inside it is deserted, but the bar is lit with strange multicoloured lights and seems to openly stock strange alcoholic drinks from another time. Dan is very keen to try them all, and especially likes the clear spirit with flakes of gold leaf floating in it - he decides to take this bottle and then hunts around in the back room for unopened bottles as well. The others load up a cart with all they can (including wine from 1982 which is very confusing) and they head to the Clover Club to stash it away.

At the Clover Club, they enter the back room only to find alternate versions of themselves - Peter Gill is partly transformed into a Deep One, Keith has embraced the power of Ithaqua with glowing blue eyes, Dan's crown is glowing and the cloak has melded with his skin, and Father Mateo is a demonic creature with red skin and smoke rising from his body.

At first the fight is pretty even, with both sides inflicting damage on the other - the tide turns when Keith casts Blinding Light which prevents the doppelgangers from dodging and enabling Peter and Dan to inflict massive damage on them. Having finished them all off and stashed the booze, Father Mateo suggests returning to the sanctity of the church to hopefully gain some safety and make sure the other survivors are still okay.

Back at the Church

Travellinng to the church, the team see their past played out in front of them as they walk, but not the same way that they remember - they see the car chase, first with them escaping, but then with them on motorbikes chasing down others in the car. They see themselves performing The King in Yellow on Stage in front of an audience of cheering corpses.

At the church, they enter and find at least four other versions of themselves (along with versions of McG and Amos) - each with a different story to tell. In some timelines, someone had used the power of the cloak and crown to release Cthulhu. In others, Adelei had merged with Ithaqua, and in others many had succumbed to transforming into deep ones and the remaining humans were in hiding or on the run. In one, someone had sacrificed themselves with the aid of the cloak and the crown to reseal Cthulhu.

Another Keith offered present-day Keith to merge to gain knowledge that may be helpful, but at the cost of their Sanity. Keith accepted, and learnt how to summon a spirit familiar. Dan and Peter also decided to undergo the ritual - each of whom gained skills at the cost of knowing more than they were meant to know.

Keith had a vision of Adelei at the Miskatonic Observatory, body wreathed in lightning and blood, completing a ritual - but no clue whether it was to bind or release Cthulhu.

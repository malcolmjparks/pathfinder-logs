Horror in High Gear

Escape from Innsmouth

With the truck loaded up, Amos takes the wheel with McG riding shotgun. Dan, Keith and Peter are in the bed of the truck keeping guard and ready to fend off attacks - which is lucky, as when they pull out of the Marsh Refinery into River Street they are immediately pursued by two motorbikes with riders on the back firing Tommy Guns at them.

They shoot one one bike to pieces, which crashes into a building as they make their escape, but after killing the shooter on the second bike Dan decides that he should jump onto the bike himself. With some excellent timing and a lot of luck, he succeeds. McG decides to "help" by shooting the driver of the bike and killing him, but Dan manages to shove the body off and take control of the bike.

Two cars attempt to intercept, one chasing from behind and one attempting to ram the truck from the side. Amos manages to manoeuvre the truck skillfully to avoid crashing and McG showers the car with bullets as Keith sets fire to everything around with his Azure Flame. Both cars are eventually put out of action.

Three more motorbikes try to intercept, and Peter attempts to duplicate Dan's feat, but does not manage - he is kicked back by the shooter of a bike, and is at risk of falling to the ground, but manages to grab hold of the truck at the last moment, desperately clinging to the side. The team do manage to kill the remaining bikers, with Dan running over the foot of one of them. They are almost out of town when the engine of the truck sputters and dies.

McG gets out and is working on the engine when they see a crowd of several hundred people heading towards them with pitchforks and torches. He manages to fix the engine quickly and Amos hastily drives away. As they are leaving Amos, Keith and Peter catch a glimpse of something huge behind them rising from the water and blocking out the stars. They can hear a series of massive crashes behind them, but they turn the corner into the woods and Innsmouth is left behind. When they get to the location where they stashed their car, they load a couple of motorbikes into the bed of the truck and bring all the vehicles back to Arkham.

The Terror of Devil Reef

At the Clover Club they bring the contraband goods and treasure to Seamus, who is pleased with the results of the excursion. Keith lets slip that there was some monstrosity back at Innsmouth, and Seamus tasks the investigators with the job of ensuring that nothing can be tied back to the O'Bannions and preferably putting a stop to whatever it is. He also tells them that it was reported on the radio that some kind of "natural disaster" has destroyed a large part of Innsmouth overnight. The team fence the treasure that they stole from the refinery and purchase some new guns and armour.

The investigators drive to Kingsport and hire Old Tom to take them round the headland and up to Devil Reef off the coast of Innsmouth (since they would definitely run into trouble of several kinds if they went back to Innsmouth directly). The journey takes a couple of hours in the fog, but as they approach Devil Reef the boat is suddenly attacked, with several tentacles grabbing the vessel and an enormous octopus head emerging from the water - though Amos knows this is much smaller than whatever they saw in the distance the previous night.

Peter manages to get a critical shot at its head, and fire seems to deal extra damage to it - Amos throws a petrol bomb which causes it a lot of damage, and Keith's Azure Flame comes into play again. Dan manages to chop two tentacles off that were grabbing the boat and before too long the beast is knocked unconscious and then finished off. Keith ponders whether to make calamari.

As they approach Devil Reef the investigators notice several tunnels uncovered at low tide. They decide to explore.

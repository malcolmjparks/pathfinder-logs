Mission 4: The Ziggurat of Alom Shallar

Journey to the Sands

The agents reach the Singing Sands, where Salah wishes them well and leaves them. The sands are bloody red, and keening cries drift over the dunes. Bruce and the agents all plug their ears and carry on. Camping that night, Yuri and Eva take the first watch, where they are attacked by snakes made of sand. Yuri wakes James, who shoots his tent in panic. After a while, the sand snakes recede.

Obsidian Past

The rest of the journey is quiet, except the hissing sound that follows them as they travel through the sands. After several hours, the agents see a 50m tall ziggurat framed against the afternoon sun. It appears to be made entirely of obsidian. They see several destroyed black sun vehicles outside, as well as a sacrificed body, which they think might have been part of a ritual to raise the ziggurat from the sands. JJ translates the carved words above the entrance, which read "Alom Shallar. Her prison and her tomb. Let none disturb her confinement. Let death take them."

Entering the ziggurat, the door suddenly closes behind them. They make their way further in, and find an antechamber with vast bas-reliefs made of marble. Eva manages to interpret what is happening.

  • Alom Shallar was born into a noble family, but one of her ancestors lost the Sceptre of Yig to Alexander the Great, leading to their house being disgraced.
  • As a child, she showed great sorcerous power and was inducted into the Order of Yig.
  • She rose through the ranks, and grew in ambition and power.
  • She plotted to overthrow the Serpent Empress, but was captured along with her retinue. They are imprisoned within this obsidian ziggurat, which was sunk beneath the sands.

Cieron touches a wet part of the bas-relief, which then lashes out at everyone there.

The Corpse Show

It takes them some time to find their way through the maze, as inside it is part illusion. Eventually they find the main chamber - four large statues of Alom Shallar surround a shattered jade sarcophagus. Everywhere there are parts of dead bodies, and every surface is covered in blood and viscera. The agents find a camera mounted on a tripod pointed at the sarcophagus. They find a projector in an alcove, and hastily set it up to watch the film.

  • Six Black Sun Canons gather around the sarcophagus, arms raised and chanting. Troopers are also nearby holding guns raised.
  • A tumult of power spreads from the Canons to the sarcophagus.
  • The sarcophagus lid explodes outwards, shattering as it does so and shearing three of the Canons into pieces.
  • Alom Shallar emerges, and the surviving Black Sun Canons speak to her, gesticulating wildly.
  • She listens, then lazily moves her arm - the Canons are raised into the air and begin to come apart in a series of hideous bloody separations.
  • A large formless shape scuttles up the wall onto the ceiling. Some troopers fire at it. The camera shows chunks of viscera and blood flying.
  • Alom Shallar's retinue now also emerge. Alom Shallar moves towards the camera.

As the film finishes, the dark formless shape drops from the ceiling. At that moment Bruce says a quick goodbye and seals the chamber behind him. The dark form - a Shuggonite - attacks, but as it is already injured the agents finish it off without too much trouble. They search around the chamber and find a notebook from the lead Black Sun Canon, and a way out through the ceiling. They make their way out, only to find Bruce gone with the truck, and all the camels shot in the head.

Mission 1: Dying to Meet You

Scene 1: A Note from an Old Friend

The agents arrive in Alexandria after being posted there by Section M. They equip themselves with some local weapons, and settle in to the city by browsing the street vendors. James receives a note from an old friend - Aisha Aziz - asking to meet him in The Rose of the Desert, a well known tea-shop. Asking around, they find their way there, though Yuri suspects that they may be being followed.

Scene 2: The Rose of the Desert

At the team-shop, they meet Aisha and settle down in a booth and order tea, sweets, and a hookah. As the conversation turns to more important business, Aisha suddenly grabs her neck and falls over. As she dies, she says "The time of the ...theuban... the snake is nigh. Seek the ziggurat beyond the Oasis of the Veiled Moon. Find Bruce Taylor, the Australian... he can show you the way. Goodbye old friend... the Tooth have claimed me at last."

Looking up, they see two snake-masked figures peering through an upper window. The agents chase after them.

Scene 3: A Rooftop Pursuit

The assassins have a head start, but the agents catch up with them after getting tangled in some clothes, though some heavies also appear to support the masked figured. JJ falls asleep. Fighting the first assassin, they try to take him alive, but he bites down on something and dies immediately. The remaining assassin makes a break for a ship in the dock and makes it onboard.

Scene 1: The Dhow

Reaching the ship, the agents find more heavies and cultists defending it. As they fight, JJ runs below deck where he knocks over an oil lamp and catches the dhow on fire. He also finds some explosives which he takes with him. On the boat above, the fight is won by the agents. With JJ coming back up and warning about the fire, they quickly explore the captain's cabin and find a hidden note - "Brothers and sisters of the Serpent's Tooth. Aisha Aziz must die! I have discovered that she is a member of the cursed Alnim. Eliminate her before she can inform others of her discovery. Your Father, the Beloved of Yig"

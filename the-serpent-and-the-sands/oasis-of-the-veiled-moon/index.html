Mission 3: Oasis of the Veiled Moon

Scene 1: Cairo

Arriving at the docks, Yuri powers his way through the crowd, the others following him. Eva has a personal item stolen by a local urchin, but James chases after them and gets the item back. Bruce says he needs to collect a bag, and gives them instructions to get supplies, camping gear, camels and a guid, and to meet before dawn the next morning.

Heading to the local Section M HQ, they meet up with the Quartermaster and JJ convinces them to part with some restricted weapons - three Blevins Enzymatic guns and a Blessed Kukri. They also suggest Chuma Salah as a possible guide and caravan leader, who the agents contact. JJ negotiates a good deal for them, and they start the journey as the sun rises. Bruce drives his truck "Sheila" out to meet them.

Scene 2: Journey to the Oasis

Yuri, Cieron and Eva decided to ride camels, with JJ and James sitting in the truck. After a few hours, Salah spots a sandstorm. The agents decide to hunker down and sit out the storm. After it blows itself out, they carry on. That evening, a group of armed men approach and are unwilling to allow foreigners to pass in the current holy time. JJ manages to convince them of their good intentions and ability to deal with any problems, and they are allowed to pass.

The next evening, they find a clump of petrified trees, and Bruce cried out "That's it!" He floors the accelerator and closes his eyes. Yuri manages to steer him camel through the anomaly, but Eva and Cieron are thrown off and have to drag their camels through. They see two pools, with some trees with hundreds of falcons resting on them.

Scene 3: Battle of the Oasis

Just before dawn, the agents are woken by deafening screeching. The falcons soar from their perches, forming a dark cloud, writhing and twisting. Individual birds break from the group and dive at the agents, swerving and flying off south and west before rising to join the throng. Salah runs to check on the camels, and Bruce heads to the north-east.

The agents spot cultists crawling towards them, and Cieron opens fire on them. Their leader Shihaab al-Abdalla strides forward and casts a spell to bind the agents with tendrils of black smoke. He also has two enormous horned vipers with him, which set about attacking the agents. Yuri escapes, and chops one of the vipers in half, but is then charmed by Shihaab and is enthralled. Cieron and Eva are hurt badly. One of the cultists goes to chop Yuri's head off, but a mysterious shot rings out and the cultist falls dead. Bruce returns and starts firing, and an explosion erupts from where he went.

Things are looking bad when more cultists appear, but at that moment a vortex of sand appears and encompasses the cultists, scouring the skin from their bodies. They make a panicked retreat, sacrificing themselves to protect Shihaab. With the cultists driven away, the vortex forms various faces and tries to communicate with the agents - Eva succeeds, and is healed by this spirit known as Bo-Ha-Bo, who then returns to her rest.

The Caravanserai of a Thousand Fragrant Sighs

Crossroads

Heading in to the Caravanserai in the morning, the agents start to look around and trade with the locals. Yuri makes conversation with one of the food vendors, who tells of the legends saying that Alexander the Great founded the caravanserai and had a vision here of conquering this world and many others.

James spots Serpent's Tooth cultists moving about and sometimes watching them. At their camp that night, Eva's koala leads them to a mysterious building in the caravanserai, where they see a cultist put his pendant in a special lock and enter a hidden door. The koala then warns them as a large number of Serpent's Tooth cultists and assassins drop down from the rooftops around them.

The battle is tough, with the sheer number of cultists threatening to overwhelm the agents - however as more cultists arrive, the agents are rescued by a new group who quickly despatch the Serpent's Tooth. Their leader introduces herself as Nabila Daher, a friend of Aisha and a leader amongst the Order of the Alnim - the Order of the Mongoose - founded many centuries ago to oppose the Serpent's Tooth and their Ophidian masters.

She tells the agents that the cultists' activities has drastically increased recently, and that they may be performing some kind of ritual tonight - a perfect opportunity to infiltrate and catch them by surprise.

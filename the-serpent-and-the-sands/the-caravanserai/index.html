The Caravanserai of a Thousand Fragrant Sighs

Crossroads

Heading in to the Caravanserai in the morning, the agents start to look around and trade with the locals. Yuri makes conversation with one of the food vendors, who tells of the legends saying that Alexander the Great founded the caravanserai and had a vision here of conquering this world and many others.

James spots Serpent's Tooth cultists moving about and sometimes watching them. At their camp that night, Eva's koala leads them to a mysterious building in the caravanserai, where they see a cultist put his pendant in a special lock and enter a hidden door. The koala then warns them as a large number of Serpent's Tooth cultists and assassins drop down from the rooftops around them.

The battle is tough, with the sheer number of cultists threatening to overwhelm the agents - however as more cultists arrive, the agents are rescued by a new group who quickly despatch the Serpent's Tooth. Their leader introduces herself as Nabila Daher, a friend of Aisha and a leader amongst the Order of the Alnim - the Order of the Mongoose - founded many centuries ago to oppose the Serpent's Tooth and their Ophidian masters. They accompany her to her caravan where they are healed and fed.

She tells the agents that the cultists' activities has drastically increased recently, and that they may be performing some kind of ritual tonight - a perfect opportunity to infiltrate and catch them by surprise.

What Slithers Below

Dahir and four of her associates come with the agents to the door that the cultists used earlier, and used a serpent pendant to open it. Inside they find a storeroom, with a recess that fits another pendant. Putting this in opens a secret wall and a staircase leads down.

Exploring the underground maze, the agents find some cultists training and decide to leave them alone. In one chamber portcullises slam down, but JJ picks the lock apart easily and they escape. In another chamber, they find a Gryphon chained to the wall and chomping on some dead goats. Eva befriends it somewhat, and everyone else sneaks through as she keeps it entertained. They hear some more cultists laughing and gambling and decide to sneak past them.

At a large library, they find a copy of In the Macedon's Footsteps tome along with other old documents that Nabila takes with her. At the far end of the library, they hear chanting coming from behind a wall. Eva works out that the symbols on the wall represent planets, and she presses them in the correct order on the second attempt, opening up another secret door and a passage beyond.

The Serpent's Lair

At the entrance to a huge chamber, the agents peek in. Inside, there is a large dais with Shihaab al-Abdalla performing a complex ritual on it. To one side, they see Alom Shallar with her retinue of Ophidians. Other cultists are bringing ritual materials from a room in the rear. About fifty other cultists are present in the room.

After a hasty tactical discussion, JJ decides to throw a bomb onto the dais. This knocks Shihaab back and disrupts the ritual - Alom Shallar retreats down a passageway with her companions, collapsing it behind her. The chamber starts to shake as Yig displays his wrath, and pieces of stone from the ceiling start tumbling down. Shihaab is furious and attacks with as much magic as he can muster. Yuri attacks back with his axe, and eventually Shihaab falls down. At that moment, several cultists grab his body and make a break for an exit, and the agents start running to escape as well. After a few hair-raising moments, they emerge into the night only to find the caravanserai collapsed behind them. Luckily the Alnim caravan was further away near the oasis, so the team heads there to get some rest.

After a night's rest, Nabila discusses the tome and documents that they found. It's clear that the tome contains crucial information on the location of the Temple of Stars, where Alexander the great hid the Sceptre of Yig that he won after battling the Ophidians. Dahir agrees to guide them to the Forsaken Tomb, which supposedly shows the way to the Temple.

With Dahir and some other members of the Alnim to accompany them, the agents take camels and supplies for two days march. On the third morning of travel, Dahir points to a hill where the Forsaken Tomb should sit - but the hilltop is empty. When they climb up, they see on the other side a massive temple structure that has risen from the sands, with a swirling sandstorm surrounding it.

Mission 2: Tobruk

The agents spend a little time in the Alexandria library as Section M try to track down Bruce Taylor. They find various pieces of information and myths, including

  • Serpent People or Ophidians have featured in many stories
  • Millennia ago a vast serpent empire once covered the globe
  • Sunken cities lay beneath the sand, waiting to be reawakened
  • Several existing cults like the Children of Typhon and the Serpent's Tooth are known to be snake worshipers of a snake god known as Yig

Back at Section M, Captain Brenda Andresson tells you that Bruce has been found - in Tubruk, currently besieged by Italian troops. Flying and boating in are not options, but a New Zealand long range desert patrol is heading that way and can drop you off nearby.

Scene 1: A Drive Across the Sand

A short hike out of Cairo, they are met by the Kiwis and their three modified patrol group vehicles. On the three-day journey, they are trained in desert survival.

125km out of Tubruk, they are stopped to refuel and hear the sound of a battle in the distance, along with a strange buzzing noise. When they arrive at the site of the battle, the victor has departed, leaving behind only mangled Italian soldiers and vehicles. Yuro thinks that the vehicles were destroyed by some sort of experimental weapon. They also spot a strange flat depression as something large moved around, but they lose the tracks when the ground becomes rocky.

Scene 2: No Man's Land

About 5km out of Tobruk, the agents say goodbye to the Kiwis who head off South. Under cover of darkness, the agents try to make their way into the city, timing their runs to avoid the searchlights. Luckily the Italians are not particularly vigilant, and they are more focussed on stopping anyone from coming out of the city. Unfortunately, Yuri steps on a mine - luckily JJ is on hand to disarm it for him.

Reaching the outskirts of the city, they are confronted by some sceptical Australian troops, but manage to talk their way in. When they mention Bruce Taylor's name, the troops have mixed feelings about him:

  • Always looking to make himself rich
  • Known for locating items of dubious legality
  • Smooth talker who normally manages to get himself out of trouble
  • Caught "deserting" and is being held prisoner - he claims he was trying to "retrieve something for the good of the British Empire" but won't say what

Scene 3: A Caged Bird

Captain Foster hands Bruce over to the agents. Bruce says he knows how to get to the Oasis of Veiled Moon, and that he can guide them. Speaking with Captain Sullivan at the port, the agents request assistance in getting out of Tubruk. He has just the man in mind - Maloud is leaving in a few hours, and his dhow will fit them comfortably. Maloud confesses to be a collector of antiquities and oddities, and requests that the agents can pay him with some unusual artifacts in the future.

At sea, a crewman calls to them that a U-boat has surfaced and is demanding they stop and prepare to be boarded. Maloud shrugs and says "I think I know the commander. Do not fire upon them - words are the best weapons. Now might be a good time to desert. You are deserters, right?"

The Oberleutnant zer See introduced herself as Hannah Sarbruck. She chats to Maloud in Berber for several minutes, and seems amused to see the agents. Yuri notices a wolfhead badge on her collar, seemingly indicating that she may be a member of NachtWolfe. She bows to Maloud and leaves.

Eventually, they reach Cairo without further incident.

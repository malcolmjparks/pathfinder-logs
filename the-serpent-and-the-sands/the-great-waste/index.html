The Great Waste

The Uncaring Desert

That night, the team recover from their woes as much as they can, and prepare for the hike across the desert. They butcher the camels and prepare the meat, and gather enough food and water for three days. They also fashion backpacks out of the saddlebags after a couple of attempts. Deciding where to go, they realise that the Black Sun journal mentioned a Caravanserai about 45 miles west of their current location, which also seems to be a base for the Serpent's Tooth. They decide to set off in the early morning.

The Deep Desert

Day One

Going is fairly easy in the morning across the sand, meaning the agents make good progress. At one point they see some Italian planes approaching, and they manage to find cover before they are shot at too much. They settle down for the night in a camp.

Day Two

The next day the agents find themselves on some hard pan, which offers some quicker going. At one point they spot a herd of game, and with some quick thinking they shoot some and spend some time butchering them. This provides extra rations in case they take longer to get to their destination.

Day Three

Entering some dry wash, the agents find a small stream, which they use to fill up their water containers. The canyons become a maze, but with some excellent navigation they find their way through and make camp again.

Day Four

As they are nearing the end of their journey, the agents are unfortunate to encounter a Black Sun squad in a truck. The troopers get out and open fire, and the Black Sun Dämonenbändiger summons his bonded Teufelshund. It is a hard battle, with several agents going down injured, but eventually they overcome the Black Sun. They take the truck and drive the last few miles to the Caravanserai of a Thousand Fragrant Sighs, where they camp by the oasis to recover.
